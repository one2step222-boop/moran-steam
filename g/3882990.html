어떤 게임인가

Follow the trails. Find the tails.Tails & Trails is a cozy 3D hidden-object game about exploring colorful little worlds and photographing the cats hiding inside them.

Find them your wayThere’s more to finding a cat than simply clicking on it.
Explore each environment freely, interact with the world and solve playful little puzzles to reach some particularly good hiding spots.

Meet the localsEvery level is filled with cats doing their own thing.
Some sleep. Some explore. Some find questionable places to sit. And some seem considerably more interested in food than whatever you're doing.

Take your timeThere are no monsters to fight and no complicated systems to learn.
Just explore, solve little puzzles, take some pictures and see if you can find every cat hiding along the trail.

Can you photograph them all? 🐾

Update 1.1.0 — Comfort & Quality of Life

한글 요약이 품질 관문에 걸려 원문만 둡니다.

원문 보기

Thanks again for all the feedback! This update focuses on making Tails & Trails more comfortable, readable, and pleasant to play. Reduced the pixel effect to improve clarity, especially at higher resolutions. Disabled Unreal’s default motion blur to help reduce motion sickness. Reduced camera flash intensity for a softer photo effect. Added a subtle glow/overlay to interactable objects so they’re easier to identify. Increased brightness in Level 5 for better visibility. Added a cat checklist: hold Tab on keyboard or Select/View on controller to see which cats you’ve already found, along with Boo and L+D. Fixed Miguel/Puzzle Cat being photographable before actually solving the puzzle. Fixed an issue where Boo sometimes couldn’t spawn in the final level. Various minor tweaks and improvements. Thanks to everyone who has been playing, reporting issues, and suggesting improvements!🐾📸
