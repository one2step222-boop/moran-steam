What inspired Astro Burn to be a bizzarre Cute-em-up ?

한글 요약이 품질 관문에 걸려 원문만 둡니다.

원문 보기

Parodius is a huge North Star for me when I was developing our indie cute-em-up Astro Burn at Beyond the pixels.Whilst on a 'research' visit yesterday to Arcade Club in Bury (Manchester), I saw this rare machine! An original Japanese imported cabinet of Parodius!!! - OMG!!!! 😱 For those of you who don't know, Parodius is a game made by Konami in the 1990s (actually first released on MSX in 1988!) and was one of the first cute'em-ups developed, where it takes the same mechanics of a shoot em up but replaces everything with absurd elements (bells, fruits, cute animals etc).But those games were often seen as "niche" back in the non-internet days. But today these games would be great and so meme-able! which is one of the many reasons I decided to make a cute-em-up like Astro Burn to help break out from the saturation of shoot em ups (aka: shmups) but also make something that is just delightfully bonkers! We all need cute-em-ups in our lives, so grab your slice now with Astro Burn!Pic: HaZ Dulull the indie developer of Astro Burn, clearly waaaaay too excited!
