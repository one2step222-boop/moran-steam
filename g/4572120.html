Dirt Clicker is a super satisfying incremental digging game where dirt is your currency & buried treasures are just a few scoops away.
Start with nothing but a spoon and a patch of dirt. Dig your way through the ground, uncover treasures and build up your fortune by investing in better tools. Unlock a growing collection of upgrades, from shovels and pickaxes to jackhammers and beyond, each helping you dig up even more dirt.

You can’t keep digging by yourself forever. Get some pals like the; Mushrooms, Frogs, Golems or any of the other creatures to keep it going while you focus on the more important stuff.

Spend your well earned dirt in a massive skill tree, you can upgrade your tools & unlock ways to strengthen your pals. Every upgrade makes digging just a little easier and faster.
