SCP: Pale Weaver is a 2D pixel-art action-adventure. After a containment breach, an anomaly escapes—moving through a sealed facility by clinging with tentacles and pulling with web-lines at high speed.

Web-line grappling & tentacle mobility: Fire a line to latch onto walls and targets, stretch the body, redirect momentum, and traverse complex terrain. Alternating tentacle grabs create a distinct “monster parkour” feel.

Temporal anomaly: Dilate time at critical moments to slip through bullet patterns and lockdowns. Death is not the end—the loop resets, while retained memory and growing abilities push everything toward an inevitable endgame.

Breakout and pursuit: Foundation guards, locked doors, hazardous test zones, and runaway entities stand in the way. Unlock new powers through exploration and carve a path deeper into the facility.
