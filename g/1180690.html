Embark on this amazing journey and enjoy the freedom and the challenges of space exploration.Spaceman is a retro-adventure game made by indie studio Conmtrast.
In this first chapter of Spaceman, you need to enter and explore the Temple of Mosha in order to rescue Sera. Just explore and keep yourself alive. That's pretty much cool as well.
