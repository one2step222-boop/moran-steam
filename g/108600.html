Project Zomboid is an open-ended zombie-infested sandbox. It asks one simple question – how will you die?
In the towns within the Knox Event Exclusion Zone our survivors must loot houses, build defences and do their utmost to delay their inevitable death day by day. No help is coming. Continued survival relies on cunning, luck, and the ability to evade a relentless horde...

Current Features· Hardcore Sandbox Zombie Survival Game with a focus on realistic survival.

· Online multiplayer survival with persistent player-run servers.

· Hundreds of zombies with swarm mechanics and in-depth visual and hearing systems.

· Full line of sight system and real-time lighting, sound and visibility mechanics. Get spotted? Fire a gun? You’re in trouble…

· Absolutely vast, and growing, map that’s loosely based on real world Kentucky. A huge area in which you explore, loot and set up your fortress.
