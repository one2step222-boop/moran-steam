어떤 게임인가

You are a tire.
Most platformers give you legs. ROLY gives you a round, heavy body that obeys physics before it obeys you. You gain speed downhill, lose it uphill, and keep rolling after you let go of the button.
The whole game is about negotiating with that.
Learn the roll
Weighted jump — hold to reach the top of the arc, release to cut it short.
Super jump — a power bar fills as you roll. Full, it sends you far higher than you should go.
Wall climbing — press into a tall wall and climb it in hops. In a narrow shaft, alternate sides to rise faster.
Gliding — a reserve of air softens your fall. It doesn't refill mid-air, so spending it early is spending it badly.
Black dust — a short-range attack that undoes the creature next to you.
