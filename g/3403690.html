Gumball Hero is a fast-paced 3D action roguelike where you play as a gumball machine fighting to survive in a mall overrun by sugar-crazy kids.
Shoot bouncing gumballs, reclaim rooms, build powerful combinations, and fight your way through dangerous stages to reach to the Candy King.
BOUNCE, RICOCHET, AND DESTROYYour gumballs are your weapons.
Shoot them across the environment, bounce them around corners, and use their unique effects to destroy enemies. Every gumball behaves differently, giving you new ways to attack and survive.

BUILD YOUR GUMBALL ARSENALCollect different gumballs and build your own pool of gums.
Freeze enemies with Mint, burn them with Pepper, slow them with Watermelon, track enemies with Homing, or create swarms of projectiles with Nesting Dolls Candy.
Combine your gumballs and find powerful combinations that fit your playstyle.
