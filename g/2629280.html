Mouse & Aiming Mouse look is now frame-rate independent. Turn speed was previously scaled by frame time, so your effective sensitivity shifted slightly as your frame rate moved. One sensitivity setting now means one feel, everywhere. Sticky aim is now controller-only. The slight aim slow-down as your crosshair passes over an enemy is a controller assist, but a small amount of it could apply to mouse input too, most noticeably in the opening moments of a session, or when a controller was connected alongside your mouse. Mouse and keyboard now ignores it entirely. Controller aim is unchanged.
