This update is about performance: the demo loads much faster and runs smoothly on weak laptops and on the Steam Deck. Plus changes to Horde mode (difficulty instead of map, a tougher curve, Challenge Altar), and rebalanced runes. Highlights The demo flies on weak machines. We measured with a low-end integrated GPU (Vega 8 class) simulated on our PC: from about 30 FPS to 60 on Low, Medium and High. On Steam Deck, Low and Medium run at 60 FPS. First launch in under 3 seconds. The first run used to stall for about 9 seconds compiling shaders. Now they compile when the game starts, behind the epilepsy warning, and the first run loads in about 3 seconds. The menu is clickable right away and the first "Play" has no hitch. Less input lag. On slow machines, the delay between pressing and seeing the action dropped from about 270 ms to about 40-60 ms. Walking Subs. Rescued Subs no longer drop on the corpse: they walk along the stage paths just off screen. Go get them and they jump to you and fly to the counter as a spark. Devlog #1 - The Abyss Goes 3D

한글 요약이 품질 관문에 걸려 원문만 둡니다.

원문 보기

Our biggest update yet: the game moves to a 3D world, gets a brand-new UI, the Atlas becomes Nodes and every weapon effect is repainted. Highlights The world is 3D. Terrain, water, grass, props and enemies all move into a world with real depth. Same ink-and-cartoon look as always, but now everything has volume and casts a shadow. The arena is a floating island. The edge of the map is no longer a starry sky: it's a rock cliff dropping into the abyss, with mist glowing in your biome's colour and loose islands drifting below. You start by climbing out of the abyss. Every run opens with a vortex under your feet that spits you out head-first; the camera lands with you and hands back control. New UI. Every menu is rebuilt in the Ink style: death summary, pause, settings, inventory, level-up, merchant, missions... And loading is now a portal tunnel instead of a still screen. The Atlas is now Nodes. A new vertical tree where going deeper means more power and more difficulty, with a cart so you can buy several nodes at once.
