어떤 게임인가

Discover, relax, create.PhotoBricks is an interactive digital gallery featuring curated photographs from around the world and letting you add your own. While all of these can be viewed in Gallery mode we built this so you can clear the bricks away and reveal the photographs for yourself.
A sanctuary from stressClearing bricks is a zen-like experience with a soothing soundscape designed to help you relax as you enjoy the calm gameplay. No twitch reflexes required, this is a game for everybody, although for those who want it, multiple challenge settings allow the difficulty to be significantly increased. For the rest of us this is a gentle game of discovery and relaxation, offering 100 beautiful and interesting photographs to reveal.
Hours of contentBeyond the photography the game includes plenty of content alongside, and as a different way for you to enjoy its tactile meditative gameplay.
