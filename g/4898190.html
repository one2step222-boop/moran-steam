The rebalance should make a fight against a creature at your level fair, and long enough that your move choices matter. Demo players will still find that fights get easy fairly quickly, because the demo stops at the starting regions. Creatures can attack twice in a turn if they have the action points. Moving costs less: 4 AP a hex on open ground, 7 in forest or mountains, 10 in swamp.
