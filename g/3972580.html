Flappy Witch is a short but enchanting adventure about a witch and her beloved cat.
When her cat is taken away by humans, the witch jumps on her broom and begins a journey to bring it back.
But as the path unfolds, she must walk a fine line between the power of love… and the temptation of dark magic.
Face dangerous foes, dodge mystical obstacles, and test your reflexes in a world filled with both wonder and shadow.
Will the witch’s heart remain pure, or will darkness consume her?

✨ Features:
• Simple yet challenging gameplay inspired by classic arcade mechanics
• Atmospheric pixel art crafted with a magical touch
• A short but emotional story about love, courage, and sacrifice
• Perfect for players who enjoy fast-paced, skill-based experiences
