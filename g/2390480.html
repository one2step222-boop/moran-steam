Beach Invasion 1945-Pacific is a World War II FPS game in which players defend fortified Pacific island beaches from invading armies of troops, tanks, and planes. Use a variety of iconic and authentic weapons from the United States and Imperial Japan of World War II, including machine guns, naval guns, and anti-aircraft guns. Fortify your defenses with mines, explosive barrels, artillery fire, and weapon up grades!
Beach Invasion 1945-Pacific, the sequel to Beach Invasion 1944, also adds for the first time the option to play co-op multiplayer with your friends. Players take on the role of American or Japanese soldiers manning machine guns and turrets to protect tropical islands of the Pacific.
