NO AI WAS USED IN THE MAKING OF THIS GAME

Tidy or Treat is a co-op organizing game about putting a Halloween shop back together before October 31st.

All of the halloween items have been pulled from the shelves and left scattered across the floor. A halloween spirit does this every year.

Pick it up, work out where it belongs, and watch the floor slowly reappear.

You Don't Have to Do This Alone Bring your friends onto the shift and split the floor between up to 4 players. Prefer working quietly? The shop is just as fun to tidy up solo.

Every run is Randomized, Test Your Luck!Use Trick Cards to your advantage and tidy up the shop faster. Just remember, you are not in control of what happens next!

Learn the TricksCall every scattered item of one sort to your hands. Light up everything belonging to the shelf you're facing. Send an armful where it goes without walking it there.
