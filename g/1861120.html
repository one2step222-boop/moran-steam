To make things more interesting, Line Time features more than one ball on some levels to add complexity and flexibility to its levels. Keep in mind the movements that will happen when the balls collide and plan your strategy accordingly.
Master conveyor belt mechanics that will move your ball in certain directions. These conveyor belt boxes have their own timelines, so you will have to carefully plan how they affect your sequence of actions.
