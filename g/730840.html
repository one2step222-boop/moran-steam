Burger Shop now gives you the ability to play Story/Expert/Extreme Mode with up to 9 simultaneous customers. In order to enable this feature, name your in-game profile xxtreme!!# where # is the number of simultaneous customers you'd like to serve. For example, to serve 5 simultaneous customers, you would name your profile xxtreme!!5 To create a new profile or rename an existing profile, click the "Click here to Change Profile" link at the bottom of the Main Menu. Credit for this feature goes to the hardcore players on the Burger Shop fan Discord who came up with the idea. They're always pushing the limits of what's possible in Burger Shop!
