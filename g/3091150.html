-----Run Higher-----
Run Higher offers you 16 exciting, unlockable levels where your goal is to climb to the top of each level as quickly as possible. Your best time is saved, allowing you to continually challenge yourself to improve.

But beware! This isn’t just a simple running game – objects constantly fall from above and roll towards you. You need to skillfully dodge them, as getting hit too often means you'll have to restart. Your survival depends on your health bar, which shows how many hits you can still take.

Fortunately, there are Three different power-ups to help you:
1. Health Refill - Restores your health bar so you can last longer.
2. Power Shield - Provides you with a shield for a few seconds that deflects all falling objects.
3. Slow Motion Power-Up – Slows down everything for a short period of time.
