어떤 게임인가

marblebuilds is a sandbox game about marble physics. You'll be playing creations and building your own.
EASY AND LIMITLESS BUILDINGPlace blocks that instantly snap to a grid. You don't need pixel-perfect alignment, complex physics, or number tweaking to make something awesome.
The canvas is infinite. Spawn hundreds of marbles and build an even bigger track!

BRING BUILDS TO LIFECreate energetic environments using velocity boosters, gravity shifters, and other dynamic blocks. Send marbles through portals, slow them down with honey, and shuffle their path with randomizers. These blocks are what makes all builds function beyond just physics.

LOGIC CIRCUITSInspired by Minecraft redstone, use the digital logic system in marblebuilds to build interactive contraptions.
