Each night, you drive suspicious passengers to their fixed destinations while listening to their words, observing their behavior, and collecting clues to uncover who, or what, they really are.While the demo offered only an early look at the game, the Early Access version continues beyond that point with additional nights, events, and a total of 4 different endings.The destination may be fixed, but the route you take is your choice.
