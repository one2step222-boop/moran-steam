Warrior Spirits is a turn-based martial arts card battler inspired by one of my favorite childhood games. I wanted to capture that feeling of constantly becoming stronger, unlocking new techniques, and creating a fighter that truly feels like your own.
As you progress through the story, you'll level up your fighters, earn stat points, collect new cards, and experiment with different builds. Maybe you'll focus on raw power and overwhelm your opponents with devastating attacks. Maybe you'll become an untouchable defensive fighter, or build around powerful energy techniques. The choice is yours.

Once you've finished the story, there's still plenty to do, keep pushing your builds further, take on another Rogue Run, test yourself against the Raid Bosses with a team of 3 fighters or battle your friends in online pvp!
