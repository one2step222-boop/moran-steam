Demo Update: Parcels Sit Right in the Trays

Hi everyone! Vectis tried the demo in a YouTube video and pointed out that parcel placement felt a bit off: a parcel would sometimes hang in the air for a moment, or end up sitting crooked. This small update finishes fixing that. Big parcels in the trays Some parcels are bigger than the inside of the trays. Until now they were put on the tray floor anyway, so the box went straight through the tray walls and covered the tray's sign. Now a parcel that fits lies inside the tray as before, a long one is turned to fit, and one that is simply too big rests across the rim, the way a real box would. {STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/46485843/1b88cf4a878cdcbe66f120f805dbdffe0a0943d1.png The hanging parcel was fixed a few updates ago: a parcel placed in the X-ray could stay where you let go of it while the game already counted it as placed. Since the last updates you can put a parcel down on a tray, the desk or the floor, and the hint now says so. Thanks to Vectis for the video, and to everyone who keeps sending feedback on the demo and on the new languages! — Alex
