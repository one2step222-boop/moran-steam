Crawling Desktop Pet is a companion-focused creature-raising game that brings an unusual little lifeform onto your Windows desktop.
It follows your cursor, crawls freely across the screen, and reacts to toys and its surroundings. Or it can simply stay by your side while you work, study, browse, or relax.

From Egg to Desktop CompanionTap the shell and hatch your first desktop companion.
Feed it, care for it, and watch it grow and evolve as new interactions and collectibles gradually become available. Your pet also produces Crystals that can be used to obtain food, upgrades, scenes, toys, and new companions.

Raise and CollectHatch different pet eggs, raise multiple companions, and switch the pet that stays with you whenever you like.
More possibilities open up as you progress. Arrange different scenes, place a variety of toys, and see how your pets react. You can also unlock achievements
