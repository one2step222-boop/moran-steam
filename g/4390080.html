어떤 게임인가

바틀드 바이 베어스(Bottled By Bears)는 포포드 곰 가족과 함께 작은 양봉장을 꾸려나가는 포근한 힐링 방치형 경영 게임입니다. 부스터 팩을 까듯 다양한 벌들을 수집하고, 날마다 예쁘게 피어나는 꽃밭을 가꾸며, 달콤한 꿀을 단지에 담아 가판대에서 판매해 보세요.

나만의 속도에 맞춰 다음과 같은 일들을 즐겨보세요:
· 다양한 벌 팩을 까서 새롭고 희귀한 벌들을 발견해 보세요. 모든 벌을 도감에 채울 수 있을까요?

· 벌집과 병입 라인을 관리해 원활한 꿀 생산을 유지하세요.

· 평판이 높아질수록 새로운 손님들을 끌어모으고 더 큰 꿀 주문을 받아보세요.

· 농장을 확장하고 꾸며보세요. 게임을 켜두지 않은 동안에도 꽃밭은 계속해서 아름답게 피어납니다.

포포드 가족과 함께 자리를 잡고, 농장을 돌보며, 마음에 쏙 드는 벌들로 벌집을 가득 채워보세요!

원문 보기

Hi everyone,We’ve been forced to delay our release. Like many other developers, we’ve been affected by the big delays in Steam’s review queue. We submitted the full game for review on Oct 16th, almost 4 weeks before launch. On Oct 30th the build was rejected because the “Join Discord” button on the main menu couldn’t be reached with a controller. Our mistake, even if it’s just a link to the discord server and not part of the game itself. We fixed it and resubmitted the same day, and we’ve been waiting ever since. That’s 19 business days in total, for a process Steam assured takes up to 7 with reviews when we started it. It hurts even more because everything else was ready. Our demo has been live since July, with 9.000 players and 100% positive reviews, and our community has been playtesting the full game all along. This has completely derailed our launch plans. To everyone who was waiting for today: we’re truly sorry. As soon as the review is done, we’ll announce the new release date. This has been an incredibly stressful week for us. We hope Steam sorts this out soon so no other developer has to go through it.Thank you for bearing 🐻 with us 💛
