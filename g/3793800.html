원문 보기

Hey everyone, I have some sad news. Pulse Drive will most likely not make its release date tomorrow. The reason is that Steam's build review process is stuck. Steam advises developers to submit builds well in advance and says a typical review takes 3 to 5 days, with up to 7 allowed in case something needs fixing. My build has been in review for 23 days. Until now I was hopeful it would clear in time, and didn't want to make this post while there was a chance I had to take it back the next day. But a few stats made the full scope of the problem clear. This is the number of releases on Steam over the last five weeks:source: LaunchRivalsThe Steam Developer community is filling up with posts about builds stuck in review. Steam's support team looks completely overwhelmed. So Pulse Drive releases when Steam support gets to it, and I can't promise any dates. It's as frustrating for me as it is for anyone. Several press messages and outreach have already gone out the door last week. So in the meantime: Demo Update Tomorrow I'm updating the demo.
