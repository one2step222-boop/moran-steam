About the GameJewelry Keeper is a cozy first-person organizing simulator set in an elegant jeweler's shop. The month's delivery has been scattered across the floor, and 3,100 rings, watches, necklaces, earrings and more must be placed in their exact marked spots before the doors open.

Every box identifies what is inside, and every display tray carries matching numbered places. Open the stock, read the label, find its showcase and turn a beautiful shop full of chaos into perfect order.

GameplayPick boxes up by the armful, open them in your hand and carry each piece to its correct place. The shop itself is your guide: lettered showcases, engraved category plates, numbered trays and two detailed floor plans help you find where everything belongs.

This shop is organized by number. Thirty rings are thirty different rings, and each one fits exactly one marked place. Complete a tray to earn
