· Ballerball - 두 팀, 무거운 공 하나, 골대 둘. 공을 쏴서 상대 골대에 넣으세요. 공을 가장 세게 밀어내는 건 폭발입니다. 골이 들어갈 때마다 골대가 이동합니다. 공을 너무 세게 때리면 핵폭발합니다.

· Clown-Off - 총이 있는 댄스 배틀. 라이벌 앞이나 스포트라이트 아래에서 춤, 브레이크댄스, 옆돌기, 방귀로 점수를 따세요. 서로 다른 동작을 이어 콤보를 만드세요. 하지만 콤보 도중에 죽으면 콤보를 잃고, 당신을 처치한 상대가 절반을 가져갑니다.

· 개인전, 팀 데스매치, 깃발 뺏기 - 클래식 모드를 모든 맵에서.

Thanks for the first matches and all your feedback! Here is the first update: Battle Royale The zone now follows map size and match time: the last shrink finishes 30 seconds before the end, and a small final circle stays until the match is over. Before, the zone could disappear before the match ended. The minimap no longer shows other living players - only the skulls of the fallen. Hiding pays off again. Gameplay Glitching under the terrain now takes you out instead of letting you play on unseen. A Ballerball that falls into water respawns at kick-off. A flag that falls into water returns to its base right away. Bots stand still once the match is over. Atomic bombs drop from crates half as often. Invulnerability (power-up and spawn protection) lasts 7.5 instead of 10 seconds. Climbing starts faster. Warmup 15 seconds, post-match break 10 seconds. The start screen no longer plays the atomic blast. Controller "Invert Y axis" and the controller sensitivity now apply to the right stick too. Servers Crates put much less load on the servers, and ZoneZero has fewer crates. Full matches on ZoneZero should run smoother. Have fun!
