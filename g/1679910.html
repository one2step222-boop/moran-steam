어떤 게임인가

Blow & Fly is a physics-based puzzle game in which you move and aim at the same time. Your owner's naughty son destroyed your aquarium, and now you're desperately in need of water! Use what little liquid is left on the floor to move around and retreat to a toilet bowl which might save your life, or... Flush you down into far more challenging realms, escaping which will require all of your agility and creativity!

There are two ways to traverse the levels in Blow & Fly: rolling along the floor and blowing water out of your mouth, thus launching yourself in the opposite direction. Every time you hit the floor, you regain a small amount of liquid, just enough to get yourself into the air again. Utilize this ability to avoid hazards such as pitfalls or spikes, and make good use of the jump pads, gravity switches, keys and water drops to reach the safe haven with water.
