Deep Sea Arena
Dive into Deep Sea Arena, an intense underwater arena shooter where deadly creatures emerge from the abyss. Battle relentless waves of deep-sea horrors, dodge ambushes, and fight to stay alive. How deep can you survive?
Two Game Modes:
· Classic: Pure survival, try to reach the highest score by going the deepest, No upgrades, just your aim, your movement.

· Expedition: A roguelite mode. Level up during the dive, pick upgrade cards, unlock abilities and earn permanent upgrades between runs.

Fast-Paced Arena Shooter:
· Ever-Descending Platform: Your battleground continuously sinks into the ocean depths, ramping up tension as you go.

· Depth-Based Highscores: The deeper you manage to survive, the higher your score.

· Escalating Difficulty: Face increasingly dangerous waves of enemies as pressure, darkness, and limited space intensify.
