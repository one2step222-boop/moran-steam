v0.8.5 Update — Chroma Key Mode Added

한글 요약이 품질 관문에 걸려 원문만 둡니다.

원문 보기

New Features Chroma Key Mode — Use the chroma key button on the top toolbar to replace the background with a solid color. Each press cycles through 3 stages. Stage 1: Hide background (character + your props + speech bubbles) Stage 2: Hide props too (character + speech bubbles) Combine it with OBS's chroma key filter to show just your avatar on stream. You can choose green, blue, magenta, or a custom color in Settings. Improvements Chat bubbles now sit right above your character's head, including while seated. The gap between the bubble and the head stays consistent when zooming in. Reduced bubble jitter caused by character animations. Added screen-edge margins so bubbles no longer overlap the top toolbar. Lowered the maximum volume of background music and weather sounds. Please share your feedback on the community board!
