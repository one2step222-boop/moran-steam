Dude Simulator: My Apologies is an open world sandbox game. It's an ordinary summer day when your friend suddenly calls and says the police have arrested him. You'll have to drop everything and head to another city to save him. Now, many adventures await you: you'll earn money, work for different people, and so on. Apart from the plot mode, in this game you can also explore various cities, find money and spend it on different things, ride cars, enter any buildings, buy something, make jokes on people around you, and so on. In general, you can do whatever can be done in real life.
