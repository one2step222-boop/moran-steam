About the GameBar Keeper is a cozy first-person organizing simulator set in a three-storey bar. The night's delivery has been scattered across the hall, gallery, lounge and barrel-vaulted cellar, and 3,235 bottles, glasses, tankards, casks, crates, chairs and candles must be put back where they belong before morning.

Every item belongs to a marked family, and every fitting carries an engraved brass reference. Read the stock, find its bay and shelf, and turn complete chaos into perfect order.GameplayPick stock up by the armful, cycle through the stack in your hands, and carry each piece to its correct place. The bar itself is your guide: gilt bay letters, shelf markings and a detailed plan help you navigate the building rather than follow a quest list.
