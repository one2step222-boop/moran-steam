어떤 게임인가

Think Fast. Shoot Faster.GlyphBound is a Logic-Action Roguelike set in a world where magic and machinery coexist. Your weapons are your "Logic" and "Reflexes." To retrieve relics sleeping deep within the ruins, you, an explorer, set off on a journey with your partner.
Key FeaturesSolve. Load. Blast. The combat system is simple yet exhilarating. Solving the grid puzzles (Nonograms) on your screen instantly charges energy for your drone. There is no time to hesitate. The enemies are relentless. Use your Intuition to fill the grid, and your Logic to generate bullets. Overwhelm them with speed.
Fight with your Partner You are not alone. Supporting your exploration is your Glyph Buddy, handling everything from energy supply to offensive attacks. The more you solve the puzzles deployed by your Glyph Buddy, the more energy is loaded, unleashing your partner's explosive attacks.
