GameplayWalk through two floors and 25 departments, pick up misplaced products and bring them to compatible shelves. From pantry goods and flowers to toys, shoes and musical instruments, every department gives you something different to put in its place. Choose a shift size from 6,000 up to 19,026 objects.

Earn in-game money by restocking products and completing shelves. Spend it on movement, carrying, handling and navigation upgrades that help you settle into your own rhythm.
