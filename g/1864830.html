Emblem of Valor is a roguelike deck-building game featureing party-based combat with multiple characters and classes.

The Shattered Realm is facing an unprecedented crisis – the emerging Voidmist is gradually eroding the barriers between realms, bringing a deadly threat of countless Void creatures. The last guardian has summoned lost heroes to make a final stand. Assemble your team and build your deck to save this shattered realm before it's too late.

Features
Choose your character from five distinct classes - Warrior, Mage, Cleric, Ranger, and Rogue. Each class features unique card types, along with class-specific abilities, allowing players to create powerful combos. You can also recruit up to two companions into your party, integrating cards from their classes into your deck.
