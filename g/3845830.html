Get ready for the next Glass Masquerade sequel! With brand new textured glass pieces, immerse yourself in the beautiful world of stained glass art. Assemble jigsaw puzzles inspired by different cultures and themes with new play modes and various fragments cuts.

· 28 jigsaw-like puzzles in stained glass style, split into 7 constellations / themes on the celestial map

· Set of unique geometric board cuts to vary your gameplay experience

· New play modes suggesting different ways to assemble a picture

· Relaxing visuals, animations & sounds

· Soundtrack by Nikita Sevalnev, the original game's composer

· Over 3-5 total hours of gameplay
