Yugo Puzzle is a simple 2D puzzle game involving jelly blocks. Drag the jellies to make them jump left and right. When two jellies of the same color touch, they merge into one. Once you have combined all jellies of the same color, you unlock the next level.

· Supports dark mode and custom color settings

· Visual hint system to help when you get stuck

· Minimalist graphics and simple levels to let you focus on the game

· Bouncy jelly blocks (a key element of the gameplay)

You can go deep in thought and enjoy it alone, or you can discuss the levels with friends.
