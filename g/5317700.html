Fix for incorrect numbers in some regions

한글 요약이 품질 관문에 걸려 원문만 둡니다.

원문 보기

Hello. We're sorry. Depending on their PC's region settings, some players saw in-game numbers that were far off from what we intended. This is fixed in today's update. What happened If your Windows number format uses a comma for decimals and a period for thousands (for example German, Spanish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese or Turkish formats), the game read some of its internal numbers incorrectly. Values such as gem prices, speeds and upgrade effects could end up 10 times larger than intended, sometimes even more. Players with other region settings were not affected. What we changed The game now reads all of its data the same way on every PC, regardless of your region or language settings. We tested the fix with many different Windows region formats to make sure every value comes out as intended. About your save Your existing save keeps working as before. If you played while affected, some of your progress may have come faster than intended. You're welcome to keep going, or start a new game to experience the balance as designed.
