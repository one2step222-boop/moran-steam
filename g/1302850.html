Lay railway tracks to connect numbered stations, uncover the island’s secrets, and meet charming animal residents. With a gentle pace, cozy art style, and delightful mini-games, it's a thoughtful escape into a world of whimsy and wonder.

Logic Puzzle GameplaySolve grid-based puzzles where each station’s number tells you how many rails must connect to it. Plan your routes carefully—rails can’t cross or overlap, and every station must be part of a single connected network. Simple to learn, deeply satisfying to master.
