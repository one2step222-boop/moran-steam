Defend the flame. Hold the siege.Ashfire Keep is an action defense roguelite where every run is a different siege: pick a class, defend the keep the game is named after, and decide, wave by wave, whether to charge the enemy or reinforce the walls.
The keep under siegeGenerations ago, Ashfire Keep was built around a flame that never went out. That flame is now the seal you must protect: if the keep falls, the run ends. You're the one standing between the village and the wave that's coming.
Combat and survivalCombat is direct: swing an axe in melee, fire arrows at mid-range, or hurl arcane bolts from a distance, depending on the class you pick. Every wave adds more enemies and elite variants; some rounds bring bosses that test both your positioning and what you've built.
