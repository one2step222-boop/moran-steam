NIGHTWARD is a first-person horror game set in Sislidere, a town in a valley where the fog never quite lifts.
For twelve years your mother went out every night and never said where. A month after she died, your phone rings at night: the caller is the Nightward Regional Hospital, closed since 1995. In her coat you find a list, her car keys and a visitor's card for the east ward. On the back, in her handwriting: “04:05. Don't be late.”
Her route is on the back page of her notebook. You drive it in her car, one sealed building at a time.
The hospital was evacuated in a single night. Something tall walks the east ward now. Hold it in your torch and it stands perfectly still. Look away, blink, turn a corner – and it is closer.
