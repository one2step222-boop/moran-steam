어떤 게임인가

In this immersive organizing simulator, your task is simple yet deeply satisfying: restore every item scattered across a sprawling shopping mall back to its correct place.

🛒 Items· Massive scale: The mall holds a total of 5,000 items. That may sound overwhelming, but there's no timer, no rush — take your time and enjoy the satisfaction of bringing order to chaos.

· Incredible variety: The mall has a wide range of shops: Fruit & Vege, Florist, Blind Box Shop, Figurine Shop, etc. Based on each item's appearance and name, return it to the correct shop and shelf where it belongs.

🗺️ MapThe mall map is your essential companion. It shows the location of every shop, the shelf layout inside each one, and exactly which items go where. Use the map to track down the rightful home for every stray item.
