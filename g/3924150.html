Locker & Door SFX Fix

한글 요약이 품질 관문에 걸려 원문만 둡니다.

원문 보기

Two small but noticeable fixes in this update.Locker Hiding MechanicFixed an issue where the doll could not detect the player inside a locker. Lockers use a local trigger collider for their volume — the doll's raycast was hitting that collider first, blocking player detection entirely. Fixed by sorting the raycast layer mask to ignore the locker volume collider.Door Sound EffectsFixed door SFX not being correctly positioned in 3D space — sounds were not coming from the right location, breaking the spatial audio immersion.Both fixes contribute to a tighter, more immersive experience. 🪆
