어떤 게임인가

Next addition to the Beach Invasion game series, this time bringing the players to defend against the greatest amphibious operation during the First World War.

In this FPS/Tower defense game, players defend the beach fortifications against invading forces who will attack your position with troops, armored cars, biplanes, planes and more! Use a variety of iconic and authentic WWI weapons stretching from machine guns to coastal artillery, and field cannons to anti-air guns.

Beach Invasion 1915-Gallipoli brings modern FPS realism to arcade style on-rail shooter game mechanics. The players can choose to play two scenarios; in the first as a Turkish soldier  defending the beaches of Gallipoli, in the second one as a British soldier defending the Suez Canal. Moreover they can play together with friends in online CO-OP gamemode.

Hello Beach Invasion fans, today we have a big announcements: The classic Beach-Invasion On-rails Shooter style of gameplay is back, replacing the not so popular regular FPS gameplay.We have read through all the reviews and noticed that the FPS style gameplay was not very popular with our fan base. The maps were not suitably designed for this gameplay, so decided to bring back the classic On-Rails shooter style gameplay just like the previous Beach Invasion games.However, we didn't ditch the FPS gameplay completely. Solo gamers can enjoy the On-rails Shooter gameplay with the Progressive and Sandbox game-modes, but the FPS gameplay is still playable with friends in the CO-OP game mode!Hope you will enjoy the update, and feel free to leave or modify your review.Other fixes:The collision model of the ocean is fixed in the Gallipoli map so it will not block from hitting enemies.In the Progressive gamemode, "Mortar Weapon" has been renamed to "Artillery Support".Some waves in the Progressive mode are updated.
