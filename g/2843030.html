Memocratie is a casual clicker that aims to spend as much money as possible while staying in power.



Imagine you are the leader of the Memocratie party and crowds of people have gathered under your office to protest. Give them money or drive them away, the choice is yours! Every decision you make will determine how long you will stay in power, so don't just throw money around recklessly.



The process of the game is quite simple, it is a clicker, where you need to gain as many points as possible, giving away money, but at the same time do not lose everything in an instant, losing the support of your people. You have to make decisions carefully, in this you will be helped by the details in the appearance of each citizen you will see on the screen. Don't forget to look in your pocket, because money in your hand quickly runs out.
