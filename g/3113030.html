In the months that the demo has been available for The Oily Depths the game has come a long way. This update brings the demo closer to the standard that the full game will ship as. Localization:The game is now localized in a number of languages that are available in the demo,French, Spanish, German, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, and Korean Art Pass:The demo now has all the art assets that will be in the full game.
