어떤 게임인가

A zombie outbreak has overrun the city of New Horseon. With access to improvised and experimental weaponry, your only goal is simple: survive for as long as you can.

Core gameplay· Continuous enemy fight. Enemies spawn nonstop around you, with spawn speed increasing every wave. The old break-between-waves structure is gone.

· XP and level-ups. Kills drop XP gems. When you level up the game pauses and you pick one of several upgrade cards: new weapons, perks, potions and more.

· Card rerolls. You can reroll the offered cards (rerolls come from permanent upgrades).

· Luck and Curse. Luck adds extra card choices and better drops. Curse makes enemies tougher but gives more XP, and it rises additionally on its own every 10 waves.

· Two modes. Default (30 minutes) and Quick (10 minutes, same 30 waves).

· Power-ups. Enemies can drop Max Ammo, Nuke, Magnet and Free XP pickups.

Hello everyone!Today I’m happy to share Version 0.9.9!This update brings a major visual overhaul, 2 new maps, 3 unique enemy types, and many bug fixes.Visual Style ChangeOne of the biggest changes in this update is the switch from the old 60-degree perspective to a full top-down style.At first, I thought the 60-degree angle would look unique and interesting, but unfortunately it didn’t turn out the way I imagined. The animation system became difficult to expand, and creating sprite sheets for every 45-degree angle was manageable, but the final result still didn’t feel satisfying.Because of that, I decided to move to a cleaner full top-down approach with simple directional rotation. I believe this gives the game a more classic and readable look. I want to try this style for a while, and if a better idea comes up in the future, I can always revisit it.New Map: Shi no RouInspired by prison environments, Shi no Rou is a smaller and more straightforward map.
