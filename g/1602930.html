2021-08-21
Updated gameplay mechanics - v1.29.6

한글 요약이 품질 관문에 걸려 원문만 둡니다.

원문 보기

Thanks to some constructive feedback we have decided to modify some of the game play mechanics: Pedro will now experience hunger/thirst only half as often as before. The tutorial video when you leave Pedro's house will now include advice on how to quickly cycle through actions using the mouse right-click button. This mechanic was previously illustrated after a few hours of game play, the philosophy was that we wanted to give the player a lot of exposure to the Spanish words in the action bar. We now realize that this may have affected the fluidity of the initial game play too much. This update also includes a typo correction (thanks GameLang). Mil gracias y buena suerte!
