Every move cost about the same, and creatures far from their usual region could hit for only 1 damage. The rebalance should make a fight against a creature at your level fair, and long enough that your move choices matter. Demo players will still find that fights get easy fairly quickly, because the demo stops at the starting regions. In the full game, creatures get stronger the farther out you go, and that's what keeps fights balanced. Fights start with 20 action points instead of 60, so the creature gets to strike back early. You still recover 40 a turn. Health is half again as high for you and every creature, and potions and healing spells heal half again as much. Creatures can attack twice in a turn if they have the action points. Moving costs less: 4 AP a hex on open ground, 7 in forest or mountains, 10 in swamp.
