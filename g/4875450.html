Daily War is an island-builder battler with a slow heartbeat and a sharp edge.
Build your island. Place tiles into a growing island fortress — spawners, defenses, and structures that fight for you. Every placement is validated and permanent until you choose to rebuild.

One war a day. Battles resolve in a daily rollover and play back as a replay you can watch and review. No grinding, no twitch reflexes — just one decision that matters.
Fly your colors. Customize your flag, climb the ladder, and make every war count.
