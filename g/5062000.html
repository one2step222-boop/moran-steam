Dwarven Outpost: Incremental is the world's first 3D open-world survival cookie clicker — an incremental game you actually walk around in. You start in an empty clearing with one tuft of grass and a pair of hands. You leave with a dwarven outpost.

Mine, chop and gather across an open 3D world: rolling hills, hundreds of scattered trees and rocks, and fifty thousand tufts of grass — every single one of them harvestable. Whatever you take flies straight into your stockpile with a satisfying pull.

Automation & Offline ProgressResearch auto-harvesting and your dwarf works the ground around him on his own, each resource at its own upgradeable pace. Walk, and the grass bursts open in your wake.
Log off and it keeps going. Production and survival run on the exact same tick offline as online, so you always come back to something new.
