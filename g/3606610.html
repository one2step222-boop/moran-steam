원문 보기

Cloud saves Fixed Steam Cloud not syncing saves made on Steam Frame. Progress now saves immediately when the headset sleeps or you quit. Saves are written more safely, and a damaged save now restores from its backup. Achievements Fixed "Achieve All" never unlocking on Steam. Heads up if you play on both PC and Steam Frame If you only play on one of them, nothing changes for you. Until now your Frame saves never reached the cloud, so your PC and Frame may hold different progress. Once sync is working, Steam keeps one and replaces the other, and may ask you which to keep. Before your first launch: On PC, copy your save folder somewhere safe: %USERPROFILE%\\AppData\\LocalLow\\The Binary Mill\\Into Black. If the wrong save wins, quit, copy the files back, and relaunch. Want to keep both separate? Turn off cloud saves for Into Black on one device (Steam Library > Into Black > Properties > General > untick "Keep games saves in the Steam Cloud"). That device keeps its own progress and never syncs. Want a clean start on one device? Delete that device's save folder before launching, and Steam will download the other device's progress.
