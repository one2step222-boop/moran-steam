Horta Maluca is a top-down action roguelite where you face waves of mutant pests, combine weapons, choose powers, and turn every run into a new character build.

BUILD YOUR LOADOUTChoose your starting weapon and find new options throughout the run. Combine copies, perform fusions, and increase the rarity of your arsenal. Powers and passive items let you create builds focused on direct damage, elemental effects, bleeding, survival, speed, or economy.
