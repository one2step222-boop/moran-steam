Inspired by true events and the chilling history of mid-20th-century psychiatric practices.Lobotomy is a first-person psychological horror experience that plunges you into the heart of a derelict psychiatric facility. You play as James, a freelance journalist driven by a tip from his colleague, William, to uncover the inhumane medical experiments conducted within the asylum’s rusted walls.

The StoryUnder the cover of night, James infiltrates the notorious "Lobotomy" facility. What started as a quest for the story of a lifetime quickly turns into a suffocating nightmare. As you navigate through the decaying hallways and locked wards, you realize the building isn’t as empty as the legends claim. Every flickering light hides a threat, and every shadow whispers a warning. You aren't just looking for a story anymore—you’re looking for a way out.
