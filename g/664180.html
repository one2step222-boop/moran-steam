IntroductionDraw puzzle is a casual puzzle game that is relaxing and challenging. Solving the puzzle by connecting squares with the same color and number to unlock pixel art. There are a variety of game modes in the game, different game modes bring different experiences. The game comes with its own editor, you will be able to create your own pixel art, generate puzzles and share with other players on Steam Workshop! The rules of connecting squares are inspired by an article in a Japanese magazine in the early days.Features· Fun and cute pixel art puzzles

· Normal mode - consisting of more than 100 puzzles from 5x5 to 25x25

· Jigsaw mode - solve some fun large puzzles consisting of 24 smaller puzzles

· Challenge mode - includes combo score system, Steam Leader boards.

· Random mode - a puzzle generated randomly by the game

· Level editor - edit your own level, support import and expo
