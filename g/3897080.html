Features:

· Master your core melee weapons and seamlessly chain your attacks by combining them with temporary firearms scavenged directly from your fallen enemies.

· Unleash your blind vengeance across unique, tailor-made levels designed around the Seven Deadly Sins, each packed with distinct thematic enemies built to break you.


· Every single theme brings the pain with brutal boss fights, relentless mini-bosses, and a massive roster of fresh enemy types to tear through.
