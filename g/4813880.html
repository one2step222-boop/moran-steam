You don't manage a datacenter from a spreadsheet. You walk it.Uptime puts you on the floor of your own cloud provider, first person, hands on the hardware. You buy the servers and carry them to the rack. You seat them with a click. You run the cable off the spool yourself, find the port, watch it lay across the floor, and power the thing on. Then you sign your first customer and find out whether any of it holds.
Underneath the floor is a real simulation. Every server, switch, cable and port is a live entity in a deterministic engine, and the network switches and routes real traffic across it. Power and cooling are budgets you can blow. Capacity is hardware that fills up. When something breaks, it breaks because the model says it should, and the fix is usually the one a real engineer would reach for.
