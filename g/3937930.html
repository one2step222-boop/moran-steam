어떤 게임인가

한 번만 결제하면 되는 5판 규칙 방치형 RPGIdle Realms: Rise는 Dungeons & Dragons 5판 SRD를 바탕으로 만든 자동 전투 방치형 RPG예요. "D&D풍"이 아니라 실제 스탯 블록 그대로예요. 생물 188종, 마법 아이템 147개, 진짜 주문, 진짜 피해 유형을 공식 오픈 라이선스 규칙에서 그대로 가져왔어요.

대상을 고르고 스타일을 고르면 전투는 알아서 진행돼요. 돌아오면 더 강해져 있어요.

Update: Thieving and Firemaking rebalance

한글 요약이 품질 관문에 걸려 원문만 둡니다.

원문 보기

This update changes how much gold Thieving and Firemaking pay. It is a big cut, so here is why. Both skills were paying far more than everything else in the game. At level 99, Thieving made about 53,000 gold per hour while combat made about 450. Firemaking was close behind. Since gathering runs alongside combat, the best way to earn gold was to ignore it, and every gold price in the game (upgrades, the Merchant, respecs) stopped meaning anything. Changed Thieving and Firemaking now pay 1.5x the gold per hour that combat pays at the same level. Mastery, class and pets still add on top. Firemaking still needs wood, so in practice it pays a bit less while you cut. Thieving now gains XP like every other production skill. Reaching level 99 used to take about 5,900 hours of training. Now it takes about 150. Fixed The Thieving gold estimate on the skill card ignored the fines for getting caught and showed more gold than you actually earned. It now shows the real average. Your gold, items and skill levels are untouched. Only the rate from now on changes. You may need to restart your Steam client if the update does not show up right away.
