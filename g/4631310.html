어떤 게임인가

"Anomaly Road" is an escape game set on a mountain road in Japan, combining horror, driving, and strange occurrences.
Unusual oncoming cars, tunnels with no end in sight, bananas by the roadside, mysterious creatures attacking you, a man suffering through a series of misfortunes, bizarre phenomena that are so strange you can only laugh... Can you withstand the chaos of various strange occurrences? Observe carefully and escape from this road where anomalies loop.
While driving, you suddenly realize you've gotten lost on a road deep in the mountains. Something's wrong with this road. As you turn a corner with a bad feeling, what awaits you...?
--Features of this work--・The final version features 61 unique anomalies.
・While modeled after some famous mountain passes in a certain prefecture in Japan, significant changes were made partway through the game.
