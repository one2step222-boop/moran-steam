Description:
You woke up in some basement and don't remember who you are or how you got here... But what you do know is that you need to get out of here...

Get out of the basement and find the answers to these questions, but be careful, it seems you are not alone here...

Game Features:
· Realistic graphics.

· Great sound design.

· Beautiful lighting.

· Tense atmosphere.

· The story is presented visually and through sounds.

...All of the above will immerse you in a horror atmosphere that will give you goosebumps....

Note:
The game has no main menu or settings... The decision not to add settings was made so that players could not turn off visual and sound effects and thereby break the atmosphere of the game... In other words - so that it was as the author intended.
