In this update, we’ve introduced a new feature: quick organism creation. We noticed that the current organism creation system was too complex and unnecessarily deep for many players. To address this, we’ve made the process simpler and faster—since in many cases, players just need a basic organism without going through all the adaptation stages. The Organism Constructor now includes a new function called “Spawn Organism.” After designing your organism, you can click this button to immediately begin the spawning process in the incubator. Once it’s complete, the organism will automatically leave the nest. Of course, the original creation method is still available for those times when you want to leave an organism in the nest to adapt to cold or heat. A huge thank you to everyone playing Microcosmum 2!
