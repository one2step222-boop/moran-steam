어떤 게임인가

Island Restoration is a cleaning game where you heal a scarred world, one island at a time. Clean up hazards, rescue cute animals, uncover secrets, and upgrade your tools as you explore diverse regions and transform ever-changing islands into beautiful paradises.
A World Scarred by PollutionThe world has been left scarred by factories and hazards. Islands were left damaged, wildlife has been driven away and places that once felt like paradises have been left desolate. No one else seems willing to do anything about it. But you do care, more than anything. So you set sail to fix what was broken and restore the world.

Restore Nature, One Island at a TimeEach island starts out damaged by hazards, but the more you clean, the more nature begins to return. Use your special cleaning tools to remove hazards, help cute animals, uncover secrets, and watch each island transform in front of you.
