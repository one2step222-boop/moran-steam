Witch's Doll is a first-person psychological horror game with realistic graphics. Beware, something is lurking in the dark. Get ready for intense jumpscares.

Story:You take the role of a young writer in search of an affordable place to live while working on the next novel. You have come across an advertisement for a very cheap apartment that is owned by an elderly woman who lost her daughter a few years ago. In this area, many people have gone missing, and the locals consider the place to be cursed.

During your first night in your new apartment, a massive storm causes the electricity to go out, and you feel the presence of something evil watching you from behind. There are strange screams coming from the elderly woman's apartment, and you feel the need to go check out what it is all about.
