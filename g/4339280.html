The joy of designing boats and ships, nothing more, nothing less.I’ve always loved ships and boats, tall ships, steamers, pre-dreadnought french monstrosities, I love em all. 
ShipShaper is a  explorative ship builder I made for the simple pleasure of shaping boats and ships. You pull, push, and drag forms into place, creating everything from humble fishing vessels to tall ships, ironclads, and early dreadnoughts.
You can create historical ships, fantasy ships, steampunk you name it.


It's a boat designer married to experimental interfaces that are intended to take away the tedium from designing boats.  No 3D skills required, no endless stacks of cubes, just some organic shaping and attaching props and functional items to create whatever ship you want.
