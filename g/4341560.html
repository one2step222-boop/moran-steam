OUT OF TIME: A DIGITAL SANCTUARY
IMPORTANT: This is a digital art installation, not a classic game. CONCEPT AND PURPOSE (KATHARSIS)Out of Time is an original meditation and art-therapeutic platform. It is designed to immerse the user in a state of “flow”, where the boundaries between time and space blur. This is not a space for sleep preparation ー it is a place for intense katharsis, emotional purification, and confrontation with one’s own process of discovery.

In a world dominated by superficial stimulation, this project offers a safe haven for the mind, based on the foundations of aesthetics and primal art therapy. Inspired by the 17th-century philosophy of Robert Fludd, this installation offers an exploration of the emerging realm of Entity: from dense, dark matter (hyle), through the abyss (abyssus) and chaos, to the ethereal function of fog (caligo).
