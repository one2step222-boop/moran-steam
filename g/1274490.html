Hello everyone! You may have noticed the absence of Green Project updates for a long time and we apologize for that, however we thought it would be useful to give you the reason. The small number of staff (2 people) and the amount of work required for this second project is such that we could not ensure the updates of Green Project while working on this 2nd project (whose name will be reveal soon).
