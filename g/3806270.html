Container Terminal SimulatorBuild and manage your own rail container terminal.
Start with a small terminal yard and gradually transform it into a modern logistics hub capable of handling large freight trains, thousands of containers, and a growing fleet of specialized equipment.
Your mission is to receive container trains, organize unloading operations, manage storage yards, expand infrastructure, and make strategic decisions that determine the efficiency and profitability of your terminal.

Operate Specialized EquipmentEvery operation within the terminal relies on dedicated container handling machinery.
Take control of:
• Forklifts
• Top container handlers
• Side container handlers
• Container trucks and trailers
• Automated gantry cranes
Each vehicle serves a specific purpose and allows you to perform different tasks across the terminal.
