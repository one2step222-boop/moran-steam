어떤 게임인가

Minimalist, Simple 3×3 Puzzles
About This GameEvery puzzle in Tricolo takes place on a 3×3 grid.
Move three colors of pieces in a fixed order and guide them all to their goals.
Pieces can block one another, so moving one piece can change the options available to the others.
Simple rules. Just nine squares.
Find the sequence of moves that gets every piece to its goal.

Expanding MechanicsAlongside the core rules, new mechanics are introduced throughout the game.
Learn how each new mechanic works, then combine it with the rules you've already learned to tackle increasingly complex puzzles.

200+ PuzzlesOver 200 puzzles to solve.
From simple challenges to puzzles that combine multiple mechanics, each one fits within the same small 3×3 grid.
