Hey, Hunters! This is a small update for Mac users only. In our big Remix announcement, we highlighted that there were problems with the Mac version of the game that we’re actively working on fixes for. This is the fruit of that work. If you right click on Relic Hunters Zero: Remix in the left hand side of your Steam library and head to “Properties > Betas,” you will see a drop down menu containing “mac_beta.” If you select that beta, you’ll change your game build to one that launches Relic Hunters Zero: Remix appropriately!
