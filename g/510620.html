Update: Major Difficulty Adjustments

한글 요약이 품질 관문에 걸려 원문만 둡니다.

원문 보기

The "THOTH Have Mercy" update is now live. We have been going through the feedback we have received, watched countless of youtube / twitch vids, and we admit that the game was indeed too hard and frustrating for it's own good. This update tackles three major points of criticism: The game is too punishing and repetitive, the game is too fast or hard to control, and the end-game is too difficult. Changelog: A "second chance" mechanic has been added. Instead of losing progress when you die in a stage, you get another chance on that same stage but with lava as walls. This feels much more fair, balanced and fun. The game is 10% slower, both player movement and enemy movement. This makes avoiding enemies easier, and puts more focus on making smart decisions in the heat of battle. The second procedural challenge has been cut down from 16 rooms to 12, and the stages have been tweaked to be a bit easier. The first four levels are much easier. The two final challenges are now "inverted mode", a remixed version of the normal 64 stages, but without checkpoints. This is intentionally super hard, but is much more manageable with the new "second chance"
