That's the whole idea, and plenty of people play for years without doing that math once. When the game ends you get a short report telling you whether you're seeing too many flops, or not betting the hands you should.

The coach runs the numbers for youEvery time the action reaches you, the coach works out your equity and tells you whether to call, fold or raise.
