Bunny Mahjo is the classic tile-matching game but in new look and feel decorated in Asian style and with asian music.
Grab your friend and complete 25 different puzzle or your own ones created in Constuctor.

Puzzle constructor
Construct any puzzle you want and play it with a friend or alone. Share your puzzles with your friends using exchange room.

- 2-players cooperative mode - you can invite a friend to your game or join them via steam invitation
- cute little environment
- Leaderboard
- no defeats

Some rules for multiplayer part
- only host can choose the puzzle or start a new game
- both host and client can use hints and shuffle the tiles
