어떤 게임인가

Starting a new life is easy. Surviving the consequences is not.
Horror Stories: Social Problems is a psychological horror game about loneliness, daily routines, and the unsettling things hiding beneath ordinary life.
You play as a man who has decided to get his life back on track. Find a job, join social media, start working out, meet new people, and maybe even find love.
During the day, take care of household chores, work at a local store, use your computer and phone, do exercise, and try to become a functioning member of society.

ALISA. Meeting her may change the protagonist’s life forever.

Features
• Psychological horror with a PSX-inspired visual style
• A combination of life simulation, exploration, and survival horror
• Daily routines and an interactive environment
• A job at a local store
• An interactive computer, phone, and social media
• Gym and workout activities
• Puzzles
