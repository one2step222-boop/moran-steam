어떤 게임인가

NEON SURGE is a fast twin-stick space shooter built around one idea: every run should feel different. Fly an open map with no walls, dash through hordes that grow minute by minute, pick 1 of 3 powers, fuse them into something absurd — and face your world's boss at 30:00.

Every ship, rebuiltAll 14 ships got a high-resolution redesign — and every one of them has unique skins that rebuild the whole ship: a samurai, a phoenix, a reaper, a kraken, a pharaoh, a queen bee, a radioactive core and many more. Each skin brings its own engine flame, bullet trail, sparks and shield rings.

Worlds built in each boss's imageEvery world is painted in its boss's colors and shapes, with living, animated backdrops and many parallax layers: a giant eye that watches you, an asteroid fortress, a crystal station, a serpent coiled around a lava planet, a spinning black hole, a hive full of wasps and more.
