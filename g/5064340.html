원문 보기

Anatomical mode: the tail no longer holds up the skeleton. The spine requirement now covers the neck, back and pelvis only. Once those are in place, the rest of the skeleton unlocks even if the tiny tail-tip vertebrae are still missing. The tail can be finished whenever you like, as before. Anatomical mode: monocles from the start, with their own guidance. The lenses are open from 0%. Until a species reaches 50% they frame only its vertebrae; after 50% every loose bone. The Tab catalogue reads "Spine monocle" / "Full monocle", you get a "Spine monocles are ready" card and a blue light path to the workshop when a species' supports are finished, and a "Full monocle unlocked" card at 50%. Turning the mode off while wearing the lens of a species below 50% returns it to its stand; an earned lens stays with you. Switching the mode on or off mid-game updates the catalogue and the stands right away, and the mode description in Settings now mentions the lenses. Free order mode is unchanged.
