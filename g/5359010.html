Welcome to Halloween Fair: Tidy Up Together, a cozy first-person cleanup game for up to four players. The lanterns are glowing, the leaves are falling, and the fair could use a little care.

A moonlit fairground. A mountain of misplaced decorations. Pick them up, find where they belong, and watch the mess shrink, one satisfying placement at a time. There are 2,048 decorations to put back in place.

Tidy up together with up to four players. Split the work, carry decorations back to their places, and make progress as a team. Every cleared corner is a shared little victory.

Earn festival sparks when you place decorations for the first time, then spend them on a roomier tray, quicker pickups, and better guidance. Upgrades help the whole team, so every bit of tidying makes the next trip easier.
