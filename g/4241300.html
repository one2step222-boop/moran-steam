Hello Everyone!As you know, our game Scrub Dudes recently launched in Early Access. To be honest, our goal with this launch was to increase the game’s reach and shape its development based on community feedback.However, since the game launched with a very low number of wishlists, it unfortunately couldn't reach a sufficient audience or achieve the growth we hoped for. As a small two-person team, instead of making an emotional decision, we believe it is more logical to focus our energy on new game concepts and updated versions of our successful titles.
