Demo update: watch your hole change colour as it grows

원문 보기

Thank you to everyone playing the demo during Next Fest. A common note from playtesters: it was hard to tell when the hole had grown big enough to eat the next kind of matter. This update answers that, and fixes a few things you reported.A new colour for every new mealEach time your hole grows big enough to eat a new kind of matter, the camera pulls back with a small jolt and the glowing ring around the hole takes a new colour. It starts violet, then moves through blue, cyan, green and yellow toward a deep orange as the hole grows larger.Between those steps the camera holds still, so you see the hole fill more of the screen with every bite.Upgrade levels at a glanceUpgrades with more than one level now carry a ring around their icon, one segment per level. Each level you buy fills a segment, so the tree shows what is left to buy without hovering.FixesA fresh install now opens in dark mode with the volume at 100%. Your saved settings stay as they are.During a run and in the upgrade tree, the mouse stays on the game's screen. If an overlay or another app releases it, the game locks it again the moment it reaches the edge. The main menu and the pause screen leave it free.Windowed
