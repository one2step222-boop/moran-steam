어떤 게임인가

GENERAL IDLER is an idle conquest game on a parallel Earth: 195 nations with their own borders, populations and strategic assets. You start with one country and a handful of cash. You finish holding the world.

THE LOOPBuild industries to earn cash. Spend cash on units to raise Army Power. Spend Army Power to take the country next door. Every conquest grows your empire, so the next one comes faster.

READ THE MAPMost countries carry a strategic asset. Breadbaskets and oil fields boost their industry. A Fortress weakens its neighbours. Strait Control opens island chains. Crossroads makes every invasion cheaper. Zoom in and watch your army mass at the border, then march in.

DOCTRINESEvery cycle opens with a doctrine: Economic Mobilization, Blitzkrieg or War of Attrition. Each one amplifies a whole family of strategic assets, so your doctrine and your route are one decision.
