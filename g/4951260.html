어떤 게임인가

Banana Kingdom is a retro strategy simulation inspired by Advance Wars and the SD Gundam strategy games.

I stripped away the complicated numbers, so the rules are simple and easy to grasp, but deep enough to keep veterans on their toes.

Randomness is pushed as close to zero as it gets. Your judgment and strategy translate directly into results.
Secure all kinds of bases, produce units, and claim victory!

A battle royale with up to 5 factions.
Nearly 1,000 battle maps, plus endless possibilities with the map editor!
Share the maps you make with the community, on your homepage, or across social media.

🌱 Colorblind support is included.
Players with color vision difficulties can play without issue.


GAME RULES
Each turn, produce units, move them into battle, and expand your territory!
Beat the enemy faction or fulfill the map's victory condition to win.
