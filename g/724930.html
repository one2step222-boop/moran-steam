Take on the role of one of the Chickenfolk, trapped beneath the Humanfolk’s endless tyranny. Break free from the chains of fate and rise as their leader - the Chicken King.
The first half of the story focuses on combat, exploration, and player growth. In the second half, you’ll lead your people as the Chicken King: reclaim your homeland, build up your village, and hold the line against wave after wave of Humanfolk assaults.
Combat takes center stage, paired with exploration. Along the way, you’ll experience wilderness survival, hire mercenaries, put Chickenfolk to work, and defend your village. As a free-form, genre-bending ARPG, Last Chickenburg features a wide array of unique interactive mechanics waiting to be discovered.
