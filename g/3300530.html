Update 0.1.11 Cleaners No more standing around: cleaners could get stuck at a litter patch, stepping back and forth without picking anything up. That's fixed, so they now clear the patch they walk to. Scattered litter gets picked up: cleaners used to go only for heaps, so single cups and wrappers dropped across the site were left all night. Now they go for any litter, nearest and messiest first. Each trip clears a wider area, and cleaners pick up faster. Litter tucked against a stall or a fence is grabbed with the litter picker instead of wasting the cleaner's time. Sick still gets priority, but only half the team goes after sick on the far side of the site. The rest keep picking up litter. Cleaners no longer pretend to tidy around a stall when there's nothing to pick up. In our tests, a busy site that used to start day 2 covered in rubbish is now almost spotless by the morning. Thank you! Thanks to everyone who has sent feedback so far, a lot of this update came straight from your reports.
