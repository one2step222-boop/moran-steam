어떤 게임인가

Fish-Click Squad! is a casual multiplayer idler where you purchase various aquatic creatures and nurture them to maturity with your friends through clicking and touching earning coins and rewards to unlock more fish and idle features!
There is nothing happier than slacking with friends!

Game FeaturesTeam up and slack together with your friend! You may join a friend's room or a stranger's room, and enjoy touching your fish in spare time!

Buy some fish fry or some strange young aquatic creatures to start your fish-farming.

Every click can gradually make your fish grow bigger and become more visible(+1s)

After touching the fish to fully-visible, remember to use the fishnet to get what you nurtured.

The fully-touched fish got by fishnet will swim freely in the viewing area.

If you are too busy at work don't have time to slack, you may hire our cute cat divers to nurture your cute fish.
