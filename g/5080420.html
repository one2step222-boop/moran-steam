Adventurers, we've squashed a bug that was blocking ability use during the tutorial and beyond.What happened: When you pressed B to open the Abilities panel and clicked "Cast," nothing happened — the ability wouldn't fire. This affected all classes and all abilities, making the "Use Your Abilities" tutorial step (step 7/9) impossible to complete.The fix: The game engine was treating the Abilities panel as a blocking overlay (like your inventory or crafting screen), which prevented the cast action from going through. Abilities now cast correctly from the Abilities panel.What to do now:If you were stuck on the tutorial, just open Abilities (B) and cast an ability next to an enemy to advance.Remember: abilities like the Warrior's Cleave hit adjacent enemies — stand next to a monster before casting for maximum effect.Thanks to the player who reported this and helped us track it down. Keep the feedback coming — it makes the dungeon better for everyone.Happy descending! ⚔️
