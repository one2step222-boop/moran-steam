원문 보기

v1.4.0 (Oct 30) New Steam Cloud saves are here. Launch the game through Steam on another PC and pick up right where you left off. Changes Saves are now stored as files. Your existing saves move over automatically the first time you launch, and the originals are kept. The game now keeps automatic save backups in the saves-backups folder: your save from right before this update is kept for good, plus one backup a day for the last two weeks. If the cloud or an update ever changes a save, you can roll it back, and progress made separately on two PCs never overwrites each other: it moves to an empty slot, or into the backups if every slot is full. The round cursor no longer pulses every time cutlery bounces, and no longer shakes or resizes with screen shake and boss-kill zooms. It stays under your hand at a steady size. Fixed Fixed the round cursor's edge turning blurry as the circle grew. Large circles now stay sharp. Fixed switching to windowed mode with Alt+Enter leaving Settings stuck on fullscreen, which locked the resolution option.
