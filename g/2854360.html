The poor communication has been one of the biggest issues of the game, but all my previous playtesters were already familiar with the game, so testing the onboarding has been a challenge. I realized that most players had absolutely no clue what the game wanted them to do, so I had to guide them through the very first level before they could enjoy the game. At the end of the day, I learned that it was a necessity to explain step-by-step what the player needs to do to overcome the very first puzzle, but they also need from the very start all the knowledge needed to finish the first level. User experience was the second issue. Some interactions were unclear, with most players overlooking important details. In this update, I tried to fix those issues.
