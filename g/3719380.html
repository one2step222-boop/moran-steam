The Memological Dungeon is a voxel-based 3D adventure. Forget about robes and swords — shoot magic at monsters with your bare hands (well, almost)! A fun journey filled with traps, meme treasures, and quirky enemies.
When swords break and shields get lost, pure magic takes the stage! In this dungeon, there are no robes or armor — just you, your aim, and magical projectiles. Blast enemies, explore the dungeons, and uncover treasures — all of which are memes! Your goal is to explore every corner and find all the memes to emerge victorious.
Key Features:
· Voxel 3D graphics and a vibrant atmosphere.

· Fight monsters and dodge deadly traps.

· Search for hidden memes with their description!

· Great soundtrack!

· Perfect for those who love shooting and laughing!
