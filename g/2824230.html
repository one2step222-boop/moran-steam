11 community translations have been released!

한글 요약이 관문에 걸려 원문만 둡니다.

원문 보기

Since the game has come out, players of Last Seen Online have messaged me asking to help with translating the game to their own language. After months of hard work from the community, coming together, debating and bug-testing... We are finally happy to announce 11 translations! Thank you everyone at 💬translations-general for all your hard work, suggestions, and help! This is my first time ever attempting this so I am learning a lot along the way. I never would have imagined people would want to translate a game I worked on – it gives me chills to think about. I am incredibly proud of everyone and I hope you can tell your friends that your name is in this game. If you want to help contribute to translating or improving on existing translations of the game, come join the Discord!
