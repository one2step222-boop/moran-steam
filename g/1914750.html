원문 보기

This patch fixes Steam Cloud saves and a number of achievement issues, and makes a small change to difficulty selection. Steam CloudSteam Cloud save syncing is now configured correctly. Profile data syncs between devices on the same Steam account.Sync happens when the game starts and when it closes. If you play on more than one device, the most recently closed session's save is the one that gets kept. Let Steam finish syncing before switching devices. AchievementsFixed the "unlock all achievements" achievement. If you've already earned everything else, it should unlock the next time you load into the city.Fixed a handful of other achievements not unlocking when their requirements were met. Previously earned progress is re-checked when you load into the city, so any missed achievements are awarded then. DifficultyOn a profile that has only ever been played on Extreme, lowering the difficulty now shows a confirmation prompt. Lowering it, even temporarily, permanently rules that profile out of the Extreme campaign achievement.The difficulty selector no longer wraps from Extreme back to Story when cycling past the highest setting.
