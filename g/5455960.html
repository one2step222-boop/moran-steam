{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/46618161/fb0c8ec8d1402b269b4fc6dd9fa3f18d443c4ceb.gif Hello from Murbong Studio. Round and Round Inc. is a game about letting all of humanity in through a single revolving door. So the more people, the more fun. Until now, the crowd limit was 200,000 people. We've now raised it to 1,000,000. How much faster is it? These figures were measured with a release build on our development PC (Ryzen 9 9950X3D, RTX 5090). 200,000 people: around 260fps at 4K (previously, the game spent 13ms per frame waiting for crowd calculations at the same crowd size) 400,000 people: around 169fps at 4K Limit set to 1,000,000: around 840,000 people moving at the same time, averaging 96fps at 1440p with the camera zoomed all the way out What changed? The crowd now moves on other CPU cores while the screen is being drawn. The game no longer waits for crowd calculations. We prepare each person's drawing data just once per calculation step and send it straight to the graphics card. Several cores share the work of sorting people into grid cells. This means each person only needs to check their immediate neighbors. People who look very small in the distance are drawn as simple dots.
