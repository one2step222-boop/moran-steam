You're a duck that shoots lasers out of its mouth, and a bunch of bugs stole your eggs. Naturally, the only reasonable thing to do is kill all of them.
DuckLaserDuck is a roguelike bullet-hell shooter where you fight through increasingly difficult hordes of bugs and critters, collecting upgrades that turn your otherwise ordinary mouth lasers into something considerably more destructive.
The goal is to get your eggs back. The method is excessive violence.
Kill bugs, get stronger, kill bugs EVEN BETTER.Start with one of four weapons, each with its own strengths and weaknesses, and collect upgrades as you make your way through the game.
