Slither Link Plus is a type of mathematical logic puzzle composed of a grid and numbers placed inside it. Based on the numerical clues, players must draw a single, continuous, closed loop along the edges of the grid.
🕹️ Rules:· On a grid of dots, connect adjacent dots with directional lines to form a closed loop.

· A number in a cell indicates how many of its surrounding edges should be part of the loop. Empty cells may have any number of surrounding lines.

· The loop must not intersect, branch, or split, and only one loop is allowed on the board.

✨ Features:· In addition to the classic square grid, this version also includes hexagonal, kite-shaped, and Cairo tiling boards — each offering unique logic patterns and solving strategies.

· Includes 5 board types and over 250 + N handcrafted levels, suitable for players of all skill levels — from beginners to puzzle masters.
