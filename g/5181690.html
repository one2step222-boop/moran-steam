어떤 게임인가

Control of the government, the media, and the police forces have been seized. Civilians are dragged from their homes. Riot squads patrol the streets. Water cannons trash anyone who resists. However, three citizens have had enough. Armed with fists, plenty to drink, and a nation's worth of wheelie bins, they're cleaning up the streets one piece of trash at a time.
Wheelie Bin Bash is a side-scrolling beat 'em up, utilizing code-based, procedural pixel art, a genre-spanning soundtrack, and a British sense of dark comedy. Seven grueling stages, and they'll bring back peace to all the rubbish goings on.

Rack up combos, hurl bins at corrupt coppers, smash through police walls, and commandeer the TWBT-01 Tactical Waste Unit for maximum municipal carnage. Feel good about recycling. Take out the trash. Do your part!
