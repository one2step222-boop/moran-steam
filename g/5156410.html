Let bacteria grow and gas swells the can; let them turn acid and the contents sour. None of it is visible from the outside. That is why the tap test - striking a can and listening to it - is still used in food factory quality control. What this game simulates is the ear on that line.Sound is the only clueEvery can on the belt looks exactly the same, whatever is wrong inside it. No bulging lid, no dent, no rust - nothing is drawn.
