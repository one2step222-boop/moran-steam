Hit one slime and it splits in two. Hit those and they split again. Keep going until the whole arena is wobbling with hundreds of bouncing, popping slimes.

Slime Splitter is a run-based pixel art incremental game you play with just your mouse. Your cursor is a spinning jelly ring that hits every slime under it on its own. Glide it through the crowd, click to smash, and pop everything before the arena overflows. Every pop drops jelly. Spend it between runs to grow stronger, then dive back in and go deeper than before.

■ Pop, split, overflow
Slimes keep spawning, and every hit splits them into smaller pieces. Clear them fast enough to climb step by step through the zone. Let the crowd pile up and the Crowd meter fills. Max it out and the run is over.
