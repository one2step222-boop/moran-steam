어떤 게임인가

이 게임은 능력치 강화와 전술 및 건물 효과를 조합하여 던전에 도전하고 자원을 획득하는 자동전투형의 게임입니다.

이제 막 신설된 우리 정착지에는 무한하게 생성되는 던전이 존재합니다.
던전은 정작치가 성장하는데 필요한 자원들을 보유한 몬스터를 무한하게 생성하고, 자원을 획득하기 위해서는 당신의 힘이 필요합니다.

던전은 당신이 원하는 난이도로 조절하여 생성할 수 있고, 원하는 난이도의 모험을 시작할 수 있습니다.
더 높은 난이도의 던전은 더 많은 자원을 보유하고 있지만 더 강력한 몬스터가 생성됩니다.

던전에서 모은 자원을 활용해 정착지를 성장시키고, 능력치를 강화하며, 전술을 연구하고 조합하여 보세요.
당신의 선택에 따라 다양한 방식의 전투방식이 생겨납니다.

전투가 불리하거나 실수가 있었다면 언제든지 정착지로 돌아오세요.
아무런 불이익 없이 다시 던전의 난이도를 조절하고, 원하는 때에 원하는 만큼 다시 도전할 수 있습니다

편리한 자동 사냥과 자유로운 도전으로 전술을 조합하고 변경하며 점점 강해져 보세요

Resource Cap System Updated

한글 요약이 품질 관문에 걸려 원문만 둡니다.

원문 보기

Previously, when the stored resources exceeded the maximum limit, the amount was forcibly reduced to the limit. This caused inconvenience, as players had to make multiple withdrawals from the bank if they had a large amount stored.Thanks to a player’s idea, you can now hold more resources than the maximum limit, removing this inconvenience. While at the maximum cap you will no longer gain additional resources, you can still withdraw from the bank and hold resources above the limit to use them freely.We are also aware of an issue where, after several hours of play, adventurers sometimes stop during combat or thieves fail to spawn in defense battles even though enemies remain. I have been playing for hours myself to track down the cause, but have not yet been able to reproduce it. I will continue investigating.For now, I have added a temporary fix to the relevant code in an attempt to prevent this bug from occurring.I apologize for any inconvenience this has caused and truly thank everyone who continues to play and support my humble game 🥹

The maximum level has increased, and the game has been updated

한글 요약이 품질 관문에 걸려 원문만 둡니다.

원문 보기

Thank you for waiting. 💦 You can now play in Hard Mode dungeons beyond level 1000, as the maximum level has been raised. From level 1000 onward, dungeon monsters gain individual abilities, and these abilities grow stronger as their level increases. Only Hard Mode allows play beyond level 1000. Disabling Hard Mode will return the cap to level 1000.Equipment above level 1000 now grants greater main stat bonuses when enhanced. Higher-level and highly-enhanced gear will now yield significantly stronger stats. From +0 to +10, the enhancement method remains unchanged, but from +10 onward, failing an enhancement has a 50% chance to damage the gear. Damaged gear can still be equipped and used but cannot be enhanced further. If you succeed despite this risk, the gear gains even greater main stats.Certain stats now have a maximum cap. As dungeon and monster levels increase, rewards and equipment stats have also scaled up, so this change was necessary. If a stat appears in orange in the Adventurer Stats UI, it means that stat has reached its maximum.Settlement Stones have been added to the right side of town. Currently, only the Left and Middle Settlement Stones are usable. The Left Settlemen
