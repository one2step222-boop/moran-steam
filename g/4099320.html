어떤 게임인가

Chess v Checkers is a mashup of chess and checkers and contains three distinct modes the player can play through against the Ai to test their skills.  
Chess v Checkers mode allows the player to play as either chess or checkers pieces and try and defeat the Ai player.
Tower Defense mode allows the player to play as either chess or checkers pieces and they must defend their king piece from incoming attacks from the other side. 
Wave Defense allows the player to choose to play as either the attacker or defender and also to play as either a checkers or chess piece. The defender must hold out for as long as they can capturing pieces to gain gold to replace lost pieces. The attacker is trying to capture the king piece of the other side or reach the other side of the board.
