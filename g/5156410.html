You will never see it. You can only hear it.

Welcome to the inspection line of a cannery. The food packed here will end up on somebody's table. Cans come down the belt; you tap each one with an inspection rod, listen to what comes back, and knock the spoiled ones into the chute.The last gate before the food reaches anyoneWhat is sealed inside each can is food. Let bacteria grow and gas swells the can; let them turn acid and the contents sour. None of it is visible from the outside. That is why the tap test - striking a can and listening to it - is still used in food factory quality control. What this game simulates is the ear on that line.Sound is the only clueEvery can on the belt looks exactly the same, whatever is wrong inside it. No bulging lid, no dent, no rust - nothing is drawn. Looking will not help you. You have to tap it and listen.
