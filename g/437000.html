We would like to apologize for the lack of elaboration on certain elements newly introduced to the game yesterday. With regards to the above new introduction, we have implemented 2 new displays for the search result. RTT: Stands for "Round Trip Time", where it measures the time taken for data to travel to and fro between 2 players. The number is represented in milliseconds. This term is also commonly known as the "ping". CQ: Stands for "Connection Quality", where it displays the stability of the connection between 2 players.
