Exitus

You have found the job you were looking for.

Each shift, subjects arrive for inspection. Confirm the case. Check the regulations. Then do what's needed.

Only concern you got is your quota.

HFU regulations may change. You don't need to know why? Be aware of your choices. Better not miss your quota. HFU is on watch, always.

Keep your head down long enough, and hope you are going to live to reach it.

Do your job, you are here for a reason.

-

Exitus is a terminal-style decision game. Pull up each case, check the subject's ID, run the required tests, then decide their fate. Every call is logged, and the quota doesn't forgive a bad one.

-
Inspired by Papers, Please and Agustina Bazterrica's novel Tender Is the Flesh.
