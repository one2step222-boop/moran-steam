Meet Me in the Well is a frantic 4-player local arena shooter. Drop in with a revolver loaded with 6 bullets from a huge arsenal of effects, spin the chamber, and fire. Every shot pulls a random round. Some deal damage, some modify players or the arena, and some just make noise.
Key Features· A chaotic local multiplayer experience for up to 4 players (no single player mode)

· Online support with Steam Remote Play Together

· 50+ unique bullets

· A revolver that randomly selects the next bullet from your chamber

· Fast paced action

· Level progression system with bullet and character unlocks

· Controller support (and recommended)

· Match settings so you can tailor the chaos to your liking
