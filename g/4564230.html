어떤 게임인가

Thousands of crystals. One cursor.
A field loads and the screen fills with crystals. You sweep your cursor across them and they burst, dropping shards that fly straight to your counter. Bank it, spend it, go again with a cursor that hits harder than it did a minute ago.


Upgrade the cursor
Every shard you bank goes back into three things: Power, how hard each strike lands, Frequency, how fast the strikes come, and Radius, how much of the field one sweep covers. Push Radius far enough and you stop aiming at crystals and start aiming at regions.



Unlock effects
Effects are not stat bumps. They are abilities that break crystals in ways your cursor cannot: lightning that jumps from crystal to crystal until half the field is gone, a storm that roams the field on its own and grinds down whatever it drifts across.

You unlock effects by finding their patterns in the deeper layers
