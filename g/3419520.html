While processing community's feedback and bug reports from over the weekend, we did come across an issue we previously faced when we've updated game engine from Unreal Engine 5.5 to Unreal Engine 5.7 a few months ago. That is - Quarantine Zone is not compatible with unofficial mods, for example mods distributed on Nexus Mods, that were made & compiled for a previous version of the game. We recommend disabling/unistalling mods until their creators are able to update the to the current version of the game. That also includes mods that act like cheat engines and/or trainers - any and all outdated mods built for previous version of the game can and will cause crashes until their respective authors update them. In the meantime we working on a follow-up patch to address more of problems encountered by players since launch of Miami update, we'll be sharing more information on it later this week.
