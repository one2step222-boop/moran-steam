어떤 게임인가

In this short puzzle game, adventure through an unexplored jungle, looking for the fabled city of gold - ElDorado!

Navigate through numerous puzzles and temples to locate all the totems and keys required to unlock the door to ElDorado.

Each series of puzzles starts with an easier "proof of concept" version, to help outline how the area/temple will work moving forward.

Difficulty:
The puzzles aren't the easiest to solve, so may not be best suited to younger children.
If you get stuck, just remember - all solutions/answers are right in front of you!

Note from the Developer:
This is my first game, and would love to hear your feedback - good or bad - it's the only way to learn.
Feel free to review the game, or jump in the Discord to help me make bigger, better games.

If you take a chance on this game, I appreciate the support, and hope you enjoy it!
