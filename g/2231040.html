The color with the smallest number of pixel occurrences was removed from a pair of colors (close in the palette), and its pixels were recolored in the remaining color. Now a new mode - color mixing - has been added. In a pair of colors, taking into account their weight (the number of pixels of a given color) is mixed and a new color appears, which is used to paint all the pixels of this pair of colors.
