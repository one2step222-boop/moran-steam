Boss of the week: Taurus, and why a line can't block her hammer

한글 요약이 품질 관문에 걸려 원문만 둡니다.

원문 보기

Boss of the week: Taurus One Zodiac boss per week, shown in motion. Week two is Taurus, the second fight of act one in HOSHIKIRI, an action match-3 roguelike built around the parry. Her hammer paints an area. When she raises it, the red covers a 4x4 patch of the board. Against Aries you blocked with a straight three along her line. Here there is no line, so a straight three has nothing to block against. What holds is a 2x2 square inside the zone. Touch the zone and it is a block; get all four cells inside and it is a perfect block. The parry still works too: an L or a cross inside the red lands it, same as on any house. How to read the fight Wait for the window. Act before the defence window opens and the blade drops on you right away. Pick the shape for the defence. Parry: an L or a cross inside the red. Block the hammer: a 2x2 square, not a line. Dodge when nothing fits. A dodge is an empty swap. It costs one step and gets you out. Spend the overclock well. Break her posture and you get one action that costs no step. Damage past the cap spills over in gold. Where the demo stands The image at the top is the moment her hammer lands on an undefended board. She ships in the free demo
