Demo update 1.08 is live. The big one: you can now play the whole demo on a controller, from naming your gym to the final bell. Controller The D-pad moves between buttons, A selects and B goes back. The right stick moves a cursor for everything else (map pins, cage hexes) and RT clicks. Dropdowns open with A, so you can pick a location, a graphics pack and your fighters on the pad. Renaming your gym opens Steam's on-screen keyboard. Fight buttons show Xbox button icons, and a small legend in the corner shows what each button does. Grapple moved to LT, and B opens Skills during a fight. Other fixes Fight settings (difficulty, damage, injuries) moved into their own Fight Settings window, so moving through the settings page on a pad can't change them by accident. Exiting a hot-seat fight now ends it the first time. No menu bar across the top of the game on Windows, even in fullscreen. Alt+Enter still works. Emoji show up properly on Steam Deck. The demo is in Steam Next Fest, Oct 19 to 26, and the full game launches Oct 28. If you play on a pad, tell me how it feels in the discussions. Steve, The Slanted Room
