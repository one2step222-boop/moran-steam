어떤 게임인가

A Game About Needle In A Haystack is a single-player, first-person game. Somewhere inside a huge haystack there is one needle. Your job is to find it.

Dig
Pull hay from the pile by hand. Every strand you take goes into your bag.

Sell
Carry a full bag to the cow and sell the hay for money.

Upgrade

Spend money in the workshop on bigger bags, faster hands and new tools:
· Pitchfork: takes a large clump of hay per swing

· Dynamite: throw it into the pile to blast out a crater

· Vacuum: sucks up hay continuously, but overheats
