Localization Update: Nine New Languages & Easier Language Switching

원문 보기

This update adds nine interface languages to Embers of Eternal Night and makes the language selector easier to find. Nine new languages: Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, German, French, Korean, and Persian. Together with Simplified Chinese and English, the game now supports 11 interface languages. Expanded in-game translations: Menus, settings, control prompts, combat UI, weapon and skill descriptions, upgrade choices, results, the Codex, hunter contracts, and map mastery. Improved Persian text display: Right-to-left text layout and a bundled font improve readability in menus and the Codex. A clearer language selector: A globe icon and a label containing “Language” now identify the selector in the upper-right corner of the main menu and in settings. Better menus on narrower screens: Added space for the language selector to reduce overlap with menu content and information panels. How to change languages: Use the selector in the upper-right corner of the main menu, or open settings from the main menu. Your choice is saved automatically. Changing languages reloads the game while keeping your existing character, map, progression, and clear records.
