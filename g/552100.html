As of today, update 1.10 is officially out of the experimental stage and live on the main branch! Here’s what this update brings to the table: Editor groups and welds Editor groups and welds are two pretty cool new features for builders. While they share a similar interface in the editor, they serve two completely different purposes: Editor groups Allow you to group any bricks together so you can select and modify them more efficiently. This could previously be done via color coding, but editor groups make it much more convenient. They have no effect outside of the editor. Welds Allow you to “weld” multiple bricks together in the editor, making them behave as if they were a single brick. Welded bricks do not require connection points and will never fall apart during gameplay. This should make building easier, reduce the number of bricks needed for connections, and allow for even more creativity. However, try to keep your weld groups small — for example, just a bumper or a door — to keep destruction physics satisfying.
