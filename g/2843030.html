The process of the game is quite simple, it is a clicker, where you need to gain as many points as possible, giving away money, but at the same time do not lose everything in an instant, losing the support of your people. You have to make decisions carefully, in this you will be helped by the details in the appearance of each citizen you will see on the screen.
