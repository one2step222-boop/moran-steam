어떤 게임인가

Cosmo Cozy Defense is a minimalist, tactical space defense game where your survival depends on your connection to the cosmos. As a captain of the Star Sparrow, you must protect the planetary core while navigating through relentless swarms of enemies.

Key Features:
· Unique Planet Link System: Your ship recharges energy by linking to the planet. Balance your distance—stay close to power up, or move away to dodge danger.

· Roguelike Progression: Level up and choose from a variety of tactical upgrades. From devastating Orbital Lasers to crowd-controlling Gravity Wells, every run offers a new strategy.

· Cozy yet Intense: Experience a "cozy tactical" atmosphere with clean, minimalist visuals combined with the thrill of bullet-hell combat.
