어떤 게임인가

In the quiet aftermath of high school graduation, one final celebration awaits. A simple walk through sunlit streets leads to an open countryside, where a beautiful river flows under the blue sky. Beyond it lies her friend’s house in a distant village — the perfect escape for one unforgettable night.

But promises to return home early are easily forgotten.

As night falls, the journey back becomes something far more sinister. Alone on the darkened path, every shadow feels alive, every sound carries unknown danger. What began as a peaceful walk slowly spirals into a terrifying descent into isolation and dread.

River Secret is a psychological horror experience that blends immersive storytelling, atmospheric dread, and intense tension. Traverse a world that shifts from serene beauty to pure nightmare as the night deepens and buried secrets rise to the surface.
