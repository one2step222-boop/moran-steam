Backrooms Maze 2D
Step into the dim, yellow halls of the Backrooms—an endless maze where finding the way out is only part of the challenge.
Explore procedurally generated corridors, search for the key, locate the exit, and avoid the entity hunting through the darkness. Every run creates a different maze, so memorizing one route will not save you the next time.
