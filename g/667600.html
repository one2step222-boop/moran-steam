Improved support for ultrawide displays!

한글 요약이 품질 관문에 걸려 원문만 둡니다.

원문 보기

A new update for Sky Force Reloaded is available. Support for 21:9 screen was improved quite a bit. All the UI, Main Menu and Stage selector now cover the whole screen. The gameplay area stays at it's previous dimensions, but the black bars have been replaced with slightly zoomed-in view of the main screen. The sides are darkened and show a bit retro, pixelated image. It's a similar approach many shmups take while being ported from arcades to widescreen. TECHNICAL NOTE: It's possible that after the update, your game will launch with incorrect resolution. Set the correct one in the options and then RESTART the game (exit and launch it again). Without it you may experience some graphical glitches on the sides of the screen. Best regards, Infinite Dreams.
