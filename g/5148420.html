About This Game"The Things We Don't See" is an anthology of ten self-contained interactive stories, each one a single bad night that spirals somewhere you didn't expect. A pair of teenagers who've cried wolf one too many times finally see something real through a neighbor's window. A courier's damaged aerocar goes down in corporate airspace with something worth killing for still in the cargo hold. A man who has watched a woman from across the street for thirty-one nights finally gets watched back.
There's no single hero and no single genre. Some nights are supernatural. Some are conspiracy thrillers. Some are just about how far an ordinary person will go once they've already gone too far to stop. What connects them is you — every story is built entirely around the choices you make in the moment, with no easy way to know which door leads out.
