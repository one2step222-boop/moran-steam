The game currently features:
· 6 distinct enemy types, each with unique patterns and challenges

· A game time tracker to monitor your progress

· A health indicator for your character

· A coin counter that increases as you defeat enemies, used to unlock and upgrade abilities

· A blood counter, which also serves as the score for each run

Choose from 9 unique abilities, each with 3 to 4 upgrade levels, allowing for powerful combinations tailored to your playstyle.
Make every choice count, adapt to the chaos, and fight to survive for as long as you can.
