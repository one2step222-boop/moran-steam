There is no finish line.[b]Endless Roadtrip[/b] is a quiet driving game about the pleasure of going somewhere without needing to arrive. Choose a practical touring car, settle into the road and watch a colorful low-poly world unfold for as long as you want.

Your road, your paceDrive manually with forgiving controls or switch on unlimited auto-drive and simply enjoy the view. There are no races to win and no crowded objectives demanding your attention.

A world that keeps unfolding One continuous procedural road carries you through ten distinct regions: coral coastlines, giant mist forests, prism dunes, cloud-high plateaus, firefly wetlands, red echo canyons, obsidian rain shores, mirror-like salt lakes, aurora tundra and windbell fields. Region order, scale and scenery are determined by the world seed, keeping journeys coherent without making them identical.

We currently have exactly one Steam review.And honestly, that made it pretty easy to read all of the feedback.The review mentioned two things that were getting in the way of the roadtrip vibe: the lack of a first-person view, and the car sometimes wandering too far while trying to overtake traffic during auto-cruise.So... I changed them.Added first-person view — press Tab to switch between camera perspectives.Removed traffic travelling in the same direction — auto-cruise should now stay on the road much more reliably, without trying to make awkward overtaking maneuvers.That’s pretty much the whole patch.I’m not really trying to turn this game into some huge commercial project. I mostly made it because I love the feeling of just being on the road — watching the scenery change, listening to the radio, and letting the world pass by for a while.If someone else can get that same feeling from the game, that’s already pretty wonderful.So, to the person who wrote our first (and currently only) review:Thank you. I read it.And yes — you can drive in first person now. :)
