About This GameMom, I’m Really Learning English is a single-player, turn-based learning card battle game designed for players who are studying College English Test Band 4 and Band 6 vocabulary. The game integrates English words, phrases, definitions, and example sentences into the card battle process, allowing players to repeatedly encounter vocabulary while building decks, summoning characters, using skills, and completing battles.
GameplayThe game uses a turn-based card battle system. Both the player and the enemy have their own HP and battle zones. Each turn, the player can choose a card from their hand and place it into a battle position, or use a magic card to influence the battlefield.
