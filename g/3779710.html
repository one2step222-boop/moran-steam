Patch Notes — Stability & Interaction Fixes This update fixes a progression blocker in the Western level, along with several VR interaction issues. Fixed Western level — progression blocker Fixed a bug that, under certain circumstances, could leave players permanently stuck near the end of the Western level, with no way to continue.
