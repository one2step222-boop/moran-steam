Other bug fixes include: - Correcting a number of colliders, so that you are less likely to get stuck in random things (selecting respawn from the menu will reset your position either way). - Addressed a bug, which resulted from the last update where some challenges could not be completed more than once without reloading the game. - Added a new menu option for skipping the intro cutscene. - Fixed a bug in the first stage where it was not tracking stage completion correctly.
