Coaster Crash Course is a roller coaster building puzzle game. In a series of different levels you are challenged to build the optimal roller coaster taking the terrain and a set of collectibles into account. Be wary of physics, though: Too high speeds combined with too tight curves or slopes and your coaster will turn into a rocket. 

The building tool is incredibly simple and intuitive and designed in a way that allows you to build your own coasters within seconds. No complicated UI, no steep learning curve, doesn't feel like work.

Build your way through various kinds of challenges spread out over different colorful environments. Along your journey new mechanics and obstacles are introduced which will require increasingly creative roller coaster designs.
