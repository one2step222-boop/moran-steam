Your pub. Your mates. One more round.
Darts With Mates is a 1–4 player first-person darts party game built for nights with friends.

Host a private pub night, throw every dart yourself, go head-to-head in 501 or High Score, or team up for 2v2.

Between throws, you're free to roam the pub, grab a drink, change the music and watch your mates either hit the double — or miss spectacularly.

· 1–4 players

· 1v1 and 2v2 team play

· KILLER 1-4 players last man standing

· CRICKET close the numbers

· Single-player against pub regulars

· 501 with double-out and High Score


· Physics-based first-person dart throwing

· Character customization

· A lively pub to explore between turns

· Optional drinking — staying sober is always an option
