This one teaches you to play them.The screen shows your equity in the hand and the pot odds you need to call. If the first is bigger than the second, calling makes money. That's the whole idea, and plenty of people play for years without doing that math once. When the game ends you get a short report telling you whether you're seeing too many flops, or not betting the hands you should.

The coach runs the numbers for youEvery time the action reaches you, the coach works out your equity and tells you whether to call, fold or raise. Hand rankings and an odds table are one tap away, which means nobody has to memorize that a flush draw gets there about 35% of the time. If you'd rather work it out yourself, switch the coach off.
