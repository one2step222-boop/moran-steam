Hey everyone, I've been listening closely to your feedback regarding the launch price of The Oily Depths. After reviewing your comments and taking a hard look at where the game stands right now, I’ve decided to permanently reduce the base price from $7.99 to $2.99. This is not a temporary sale — this is the new standard price moving forward. My goal is for every player to feel like they got a great value out of their experience. Right now, I believe this new price better reflects the scope and length of the game. For those who previously purchased the game: since The Oily Depths is a shorter experience, most players should still fall within Steam’s standard refund window. Thank you to everyone for the support, feedback, and honesty. I really appreciate it. See you in Nineveh, Ryan

In the months that the demo has been available for The Oily Depths the game has come a long way. This update brings the demo closer to the standard that the full game will ship as. Localization:The game is now localized in a number of languages that are available in the demo,French, Spanish, German, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, and Korean Art Pass:The demo now has all the art assets that will be in the full game. Game Settings:By popular request there is now an Invert-Y Look option in the settings and also a way to adjust brightness. I wished these were in for the first launch for the demo, but at least I am now able to make the game accessible to more players. Sounds:There has been a pass to make the sounds of the ship much more immersive. For an atmospheric horror game like The Oily Depths, sound plays a huge roll and I think this extra layer of polish will make for a more engrossing experience. If you have not already, please Wishlist the game and I hope to see you in the depths.-Ryan
