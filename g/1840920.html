SEARCH ALL - FOXES is a game in the genre of "hidden object", in which you need to find all the foxes on a large location drawn in the style of "doodling". The location shows a Chinese city. In the foreground of the location there is a hypermarket with parking for cars, a small pond and a shopping area (market), and two helicopters are flying in the sky. In the middle of the location there is a long bridge for a high-speed train. Behind the bridge, there is a megalopolis, that is, high multi-storey buildings. On one of these houses sits a giant ferocious fox. Behind the multi-storey buildings, you can see a building from which harmful smoke rises into the sky.

Key Features:
- Animation elements on location;
- "Doodling" design style;
- 100 foxes to find;
- Pleasant soundtrack:
- The gameplay can be interrupted at any time, and the game will automatically save the result.
