Hi, this update adds a map. This has been requested by some people and will be especially useful if you intend to clear all the coins. By default the map shows where there are coins left as well as your placed oxygen generators and beacons. There is an option under the gameplay section in the settings to turn on showing treasure chests and caves on the map (for areas you have explored) for anyone that has a trouble finding the chests or caves. By default the map is opened by pressing M when using keyboard and mouse or by pressing the right joystick on a controller. This can be changed in the keybinds section in the settings.
