Too Well Hidden is a short first-person horror game, seen through the lens of a VHS camera. It is a psychological horror walking simulator, where as you explore a confined environment, strange things begin to happen in a tense hide and seek story, where the house itself becomes unsettling.

Ana and Clara are two sisters who usually play hide and seek inside the house at night, using their mother's VHS camera. Ana was never good at hiding, always being quickly found by her older sister.
But now something feels wrong—Ana seems to be hiding so well. What begins as a fun recording turns into a disturbing search, as something else is also hiding in the darkness.

FEATURES 
· Immersive VHS Aesthetic: Authentic analog visuals, tape glitches, and environmental distortion recreate the eerie realism of 1990s home videos.

· A tense hide-and-seek story where the house itself becomes unsettling.
