어떤 게임인가

Welcome to the Fish Placement Department!
The fish keep arriving. Each one has an idea of home. Your job is to find the tank that actually fits.

Read the fish's request, inspect three aquariums, and choose the right one. A floor pattern, a low plant, a shell, an arch, or another small detail can make the difference. Later requests ask you to notice what a tank must not contain, too.

Audit shifts turn the task around: the fish are already in their tanks, and you must find the tank with a fish whose request is not met.

Read the clues. Find the right home.
· Read the signs. Match each request to the visible floor and objects in a tank.

· Keep the arrival belt moving. Place fish in sequence and build a clean combo.

· Explore 50 handcrafted shifts. Every shift presents three distinct aquariums. Complete all 50 on each of the four difficulties for 200 shift-and-difficulty completions.

Wrong Tank! now supports 17 interface languages. This update adds 15 languages alongside English and Hungarian, with revised wording and text layouts throughout the game. What changed Added Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Thai, German, French, Brazilian Portuguese, Spanish (Spain), Spanish (Latin America), Russian, Ukrainian, Polish, Turkish and Italian. Reworked text across menus, instructions, achievements and aquarium dialogue, including further improvements to English and Hungarian. Added pixel fonts suited to the different writing systems, with text sizes adjusted for each language. Language selection is available directly from the main menu, with a button that matches the rest of the interface. Improved text fitting in buttons, achievement cards and menu panels, including the shift and star counters. To change languages, open Languages in the main menu. Your existing progress carries over. Thanks for the feedback in advance. If a line sounds odd or a label still looks cramped on your screen, please post the language, screen resolution and a screenshot in the Steam discussions. That makes it much easier to track down. Have fun!
