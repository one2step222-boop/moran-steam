“A routine call turns into a nightmare at the door at the foot of the cliff.”

On your way to answer a call on a stormy night, you crash your car. Your body is left in the wreckage; Death is about to take your soul to Limbo. Just as you are about to cross over, a very dark entity intervenes: the Demon. It defies Death’s judgment, keeps your soul from passing on, and forces you to stand watch in a cursed house.

During your shift, which begins at 8:00 p.m., you watch the house’s eight rooms through cameras. Spot the details that distort reality, choose the right room, and report anomalies. Every mistake you make before 6:00 a.m. can tighten the house’s grip. When morning comes, the end of your shift may not mean freedom.
