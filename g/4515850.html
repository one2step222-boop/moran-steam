어떤 게임인가

A fully pixelated city? You just graduated from Pixel University, and you still need to pay your student loans. Can you find your way to survive in this Pixel City? You will have to find a job to keep life going!

SURVIVING

Manage your sleep, hunger, and thirst status, try to survive in this city!

WORKING

Take on various job as your working experience progresses, including construction work, being a chef, a delivery person, a taxi driver and more!

BUYING

A dedicated inventory system for you to buy clothes, food, tools... and cars!

EXPLORING

There are parks, bank, shops, racing circuits in the city map for you to explore and find!
