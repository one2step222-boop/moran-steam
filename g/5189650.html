원문 보기

More time to react to thunderstorms Lightning strikes now give you 1.8 seconds to react at any game speed. Fewer warnings appear at once, and dragging a warning no longer triggers other actions. Correct layering when rotating the view Towers and fish now draw in the correct order as you rotate the camera, fixing cases where towers were obscured from the opposite side. Clearer mod targets and automatic pause Selecting a mod shows “Can equip mod” above compatible towers before you hover over them. You can also click a label to equip it. Combat pauses while you choose a target and resumes after equipping or cancelling. If tactical pause was already active, it stays active. More improvements Clearer tower descriptions — Short summaries now cover the essentials, with full effects in the detailed descriptions. We removed conflicting evolution stats and separated the lobster tower’s attack, breakwater rewards, and nearby-breakwater bonuses. Eel towers resist enemy eel paralysis — Eel-family towers are now immune to contact paralysis from enemy electric eels. Paralysis and binding from other sources still apply. Retry the current wave with Fisher’s Regret — Choose to retry, go back 5 waves
