어떤 게임인가

"Anomalous Railroad Crossing" is an escape game where you search for strange happenings, set at the most famous railroad crossing in Japan.
Various strange happenings occur, from horrifying, beautiful, and amusing, to large-scale happenings that affect the entire city, so observe carefully and escape from the world invaded by strange happenings.

～
You suddenly realize that you are standing on a road leading to a railroad crossing with a view of the sea.
・First, head towards the railroad crossing.
・Make sure there is no strange happening.
・Make the right choice to escape from the looping world.

--Features of this work--・This is a sequel to the previous work "Anomalous Crossing - Shibuya -", which was a hot topic in the game commentary community.
・There is no direct connection to the previous work, so it is no problem to start with this work.
・The difficulty level is easy to play.
