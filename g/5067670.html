원문 보기

Thanks for reporting the pulsing background hum. This patch removes that continuous sound and gives you more control over the game's audio. Removed the pulsing ambient hum that continued during runs even with music turned off. Added separate Music and Sound Effects volume sliders in Settings, alongside the existing master volume. The Sound Effects slider controls combat sounds, powers and button feedback. Set it to zero to mute them without muting the music. Audio levels are saved between launches. Existing progress and your previous master volume and music on/off settings are preserved. Thank you for the feedback and for helping me improve Swarmvore!
