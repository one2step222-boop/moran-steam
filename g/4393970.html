In Sweetie Candy Maze: White Chocolate:

• 50 levels of candy-filled fun
• Bright and tasty visuals
• Gradually increasing difficulty
• Addictive and cheerful gameplay
• Relaxing music
• Achievements
• Simple controls

Use Gamepads (Xbox One or Xbox 360), or keyboard (WASD or arrow keys) to navigate through the different mazes!
