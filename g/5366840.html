Your goal is to restore stunning illustrations that have been divided into equal pieces. Rotate each piece and place it in the correct position to reveal the complete image.
Test your logic and attention to detail as you solve increasingly challenging puzzles. With every completed level, you'll unlock a new artwork and discover more of the Chronime world.
Whether you're looking for a relaxing pastime or enjoy visual puzzle games, Chronime Puzzle: Horses offers a calm and rewarding experience accompanied by soothing music.
