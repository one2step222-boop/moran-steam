FussyCraft Survival: Sandbox is a darkly funny 2D pixel survival sandbox where you start as an ordinary peasant in a land squeezed dry by a greedy king.
Every seventh day, the royal collectors arrive. They take your meat and mining materials. If you have no meat, you are done. If you have supplies, you survive, but the king still takes his cut. So you must mine, build, craft, cook, fight and carefully manage everything you find.
Start from nothing. Get your first tools, build a safe place, cook meat, learn spells, upgrade perks and prepare for nights full of strange creatures. The world is block-based, destructible and dangerous. Every piece of ore, every bit of meat and every wall you build can decide whether you survive the next royal levy.
