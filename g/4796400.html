Color Blocks is a precision platformer built around one simple rule: you only stand on platforms that match your color.
Play solo or team up with up to four players. Everyone is linked by physics-based ropes, so every jump, fall, and color swap affects the whole group. Coordinate color changes, pull each other across gaps, and master launch pads, checkpoints, and optional rising lava to reach the flag — as fast as you can.

· Overlapping platforms blend into new colors — teamwork opens routes no single player can use alone

· Ejection — change color inside a platform? Physics kicks you out. Use it or fight it
