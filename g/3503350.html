Welcome to Stick N' Sheep, the wildly chaotic multiplayer party game where up to 8 sheep battle it out in a whirlwind of crazy mini-games!
· Forget the game board, this is pure, fast-paced, and hilarious action. Whether you're flying solo or teaming up, take on friends (or foes) in a wide variety of mini-games: from reflex tests to team chaos, unexpected twists, and much more.

· Customize your sheep with loads of wacky outfits and unlock even more as you play. From rainbow mohawks to inflatable suits, show off your woolly style with pride!

· With its colorful 3D cartoon visuals and a tone that swings between absurd humor and fierce competition, Stick N' Sheep is made for game nights, belly laughs, and backstabbing between two fluffy rivals.

Get ready to slide, jump, charge, fall… and bleat your way to victory!
