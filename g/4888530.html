Your reports and reviews have been incredibly useful, and this first update is built almost entirely from them. Here's what changed. Economy & pricing Economy fares now really matter. Raising your economy fare used to cost you almost no passengers, so maxing it out was free money. Higher fares now lose you noticeably more passengers, and discounts win you more, drawing extra travellers onto the route. Rival airlines now compete on price. AI airlines used to sit on a flat economy fare. They now adjust their fares to the route and react to the competition. Removed the "market ≈ X/mo" estimate from the New/Edit Route screens. It didn't reflect what you'd actually earn. CEO career The world now carries over when you change jobs.
