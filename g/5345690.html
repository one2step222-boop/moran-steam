Move, dodge, use abilities, and pass the bomb before time runs out. As players are eliminated, the remaining survivors continue fighting until only one player remains.
Each match features random abilities that can change the situation. Dash away from danger, stop time, become invisible, pull enemies toward you, and use interactive elements in the arena to outplay your opponents.
