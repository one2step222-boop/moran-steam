어떤 게임인가

A nostalgic road trip on an endless highway, driving iconic '80s cars through a neon-filled paradise. Let the synthwave/retrowave soundtrack guide you toward the horizon, leading you into a world of nostalgic vibes.

The road stretches infinitely, taking you back to a simpler time, where palm trees sway gently in the warm breeze. As the rhythm takes over, you enter a state of serene reflection, racing through a universe where time is measured in moments of calm and nostalgia.

As you accelerate, you'll unlock new worlds and over 15 iconic vehicles, each offering customization options and upgrades. For competitive players, each world features an online leaderboard, allowing you to compete globally or with friends for the highest scores.
