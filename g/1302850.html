Tails of Trainspot is a relaxing logic puzzle game set on the peaceful Packan Islands. Lay railway tracks to connect numbered stations, uncover the island’s secrets, and meet charming animal residents. With a gentle pace, cozy art style, and delightful mini-games, it's a thoughtful escape into a world of whimsy and wonder.

Logic Puzzle GameplaySolve grid-based puzzles where each station’s number tells you how many rails must connect to it. Plan your routes carefully—rails can’t cross or overlap, and every station must be part of a single connected network. Simple to learn, deeply satisfying to master.

A World Full of StoriesAs you explore the islands, you'll meet curious animal residents and uncover hidden myths buried in ruins. The Packan Islands are more than just a puzzle board—they’re a living world filled with character and charm.
