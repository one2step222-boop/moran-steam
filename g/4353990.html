The Lyra SignalA quiet signal. A forgotten voice. A mystery that echoes across time.
The Lyra Signal is a story-driven visual novel focused on atmosphere, mystery, and emotional discovery.
You follow Leo, a young man grappling with loss after the death of his parents. While sorting through remnants of the past, he uncovers an old cassette recording containing his father's voice and a name that refuses to fade: Lyra.
What begins as an attempt to understand that final message slowly draws Leo toward a signal that feels both distant and unnervingly close.
As Leo investigates further with the help of his friends, fragmented messages and moments begin to surface. Memories, perception, and reality blur together, leaving him unsure whether what he is chasing is truth, coincidence, or something that should have remained silent.
Gameplay is designed as a linear, choice-light visual novel. Players
