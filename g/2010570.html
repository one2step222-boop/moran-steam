Me Alone Reissue is a 3D first-person shooter set in apocalypse land! Many people have turned into monsters that eat the survivors. At the moment when the end of the world happened, the main character was working at a gas station far from the city. Being the only survivor, he will have to defend himself from zombies in a house next to a gas station. There are 6 types of weapons in your arsenal, which will open as you progress. Stay alive as long as possible! God bless you.
