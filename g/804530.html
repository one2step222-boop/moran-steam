Update 1.8.1

원문 보기

Update 1.8.1 is a long overdue update that brings in many of the quality of life and maintenance changes from the Quest version of the game to Steam. The main improvement under the hood is a better implementation of OpenXR support, which allowed us to consolidate support for different OpenXR runtimes into one build, meaning there is no longer a need for the two different launch options we had before.
