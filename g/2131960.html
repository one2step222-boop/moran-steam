Step into the shoes of RunBean, an astronaut who landed on a hazardous planet that tries its best to kill the intruder! The longer you stay alive, the higher will be your score, and the more envious the next poor space travelers visiting this planet will be.

Staying true to the arcade genre, this menacing ecosystem generates all the obstacles on the fly.
