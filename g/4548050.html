Imperial Wager: The Shattered Crown of Augustus is a minimalist political simulation designed to capture the volatile, grinding reality of ruling a fractured realm across decades, or even centuries. The immense complexity of medieval statecraft is distilled into games of chance, spatial puzzles, and calculated delegation.
The sprawling logistics of marching armies, quelling riots, and collecting tithes are abstracted into stamping geometric mandate cards onto a territorial grid. Operating from your Regent’s Keep, your authority is enacted by interacting with ancient devices, curating the psychological makeup of your cabinet, and manipulating the realm through layered cardplay.
