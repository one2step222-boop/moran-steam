원문 보기

Hi everyone!First of all I'd like to thank everyone who has played Fellwood so far.A lot of players (rightfully so) called out that having a single building was better than having multiple buildings and that the prestige points were easiest to gain by farming the beginning stages of the game, so this update is to hopefully fix these 2 issues as well as a few other smaller bugs.Single Building Damage ChangesThe main culprit for this was the axe building, due to the compounding effects that having a high ricochet count allowed. So I did have to slightly reduce the max ricochet count. After more thorough testing it was due to the level up options that made the axe so much stronger, it compounded its benefits much better than any other projectile. I went back and forth on how to handle this, but I decided on adding a new multiplier called "Building Diversity" (you can see it in the top left hand corner of the level up dialog, and can hover it for more information).Essentially now the stat rolls are effected by the number of projectile buildings you have. It ranges from 75% when you only have a single projectile building, to 150% when you have all the projectile buildings
